# Managing Leads in the Mobile App

- Category: `Mobile Apps`

## Body

## Introduction

The Lofty mobile app allows you to manage your leads on the go quickly.

## Summary

- People Page
- Adding a Lead
- Managing a Lead

## People Page

The people page in the Lofty mobile app is very similar to the people page in the web CRM.

Select the People icon and click Leads to view your leads. The leads in the mobile app are identical to those in the web CRM.

## Adding a Lead

Click the + icon to add a lead via the Lofty mobile app.

The Add a Lead will open to allow you to add a new lead via the Lofty mobile app. This lead will also be available in your web CRM.

Here, you have the option to enter all of the lead details that are also available in the web CRM. You can also import your phone Contacts into the Lofty App using the option below.

## Managing a Lead

Click on a lead to view the lead's detail page.

Click on any of the sections to edit or update the lead's details. Click the Phone , Mail , or Text icons at the bottom to contact the lead directly. Click the + icon to quickly add a note, task, appointment, and other features to the lead.
