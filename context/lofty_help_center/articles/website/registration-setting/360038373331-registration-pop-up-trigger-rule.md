## Body

## Introduction

The "Registration Pop-up Trigger Rule" refers to the ability that you have as a Lofty user to customize exactly when the forced registration pop-up will appear on your Lofty IDX website to capture lead information.

## Summary

- Edit Trigger Rule Higher Conversion Higher Quality Custom

## Edit Trigger Rule

To edit the triggering criteria, navigate to CMS > Settings > Lead Capture > Registration Pop-up Trigger Rule :

Three options are available for you to choose from.

### Higher Conversion

This is the recommended option. Based on data accumulated from Lofty-built websites, this strategy has typically resulted in higher levels of lead conversion (meaning more will register).

### Higher Quality

Alternatively, you can choose this option which will give a little more browsing time and will typically result in higher quality leads.

### Custom

Use this option if you would like to personalize your registration criteria.
