## Introduction

The "Sell Page" is for capturing seller leads who have the intention of selling their house. This is different from the Home Evaluation block because it does not provide an estimated value of the property. Instead, it just collects the lead's information for the additional follow-up to take place.

## Sell Form

The first part of this page is made up of a form block called "Sell Form." More details regarding this block and the settings that can be adjusted within it can be found in this related article: Form Block .
