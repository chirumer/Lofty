## Introduction

This article walks Office Owners and Admin through initiating the office websit e prior to the setup stage.

### Office Websites

- One Office website is included with the purchase of an Office / Team Add-On.
- Site Owner : Office Owner
- Site Level : Office
- Site Type : SEO Able to configure a vanity domain. Has full SEO capabilities and personalization. Must go through an individual MLS application to get the data connected Websites nestled beneath an Office website will be copies of the Office website.

To initiate your Office Website, go to your Office Settings > Organization Hierarchy > Office

Choose the Build Your Office Website option.

Note: If no Office Owner is assigned, you must assign one before proceeding. Otherwise, you will receive the error below and won’t be able to build the website.

Choose how you want to create your Office SEO Website

#### Option 1: Generated With AI

- Build your website through a guided, chat-based experience (covered in this article) .
- Read More about How to Create Your Website with the Website Building Agent

#### Option 2: Choose a Template

- Select and customize a template manually

## What Happens Next

After initiating the Office Website, your Office Website is ready to be created! See our article Starting your Lofty IDX Website for details on:

- Choosing your template
- Setting up your vanity domain
- Applying for your MLS
