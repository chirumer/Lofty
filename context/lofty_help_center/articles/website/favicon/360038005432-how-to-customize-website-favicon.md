## Editing the Favicon

To edit this on a Lofty-built website, go to the CMS Tool > Settings > Basic Info > Favicon > Upload an image . Lofty recommends an image of 40x40px in JPG format. Once uploaded and then saved, this favicon will display for the website instead of the standard Lofty logo.
