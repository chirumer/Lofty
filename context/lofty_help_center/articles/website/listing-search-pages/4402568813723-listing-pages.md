## Body

## Introduction

Listing Pages are pages on Lofty-built IDX websites that display a certain selection of listings. This is one of the main reasons for your clients to visit your site, so you must know how to set up the best experience for your visitors. This article explains how to customize what listings are displayed and when using a Listing Page.

When building a Listing Page, it has a built-in Listing Search block that cannot be removed.

## Standard Listing Pages

The following are standard listing pages that are built into your website, but you can also add new listing pages.

- Featured Listings
- All Listings
- Sold Listings (if applicable)

*IMPORTANT : You will be unable to remove any blocks on a standard "Listing" page.

To edit one of these pages, simply navigate to CMS > Page Editor > Page Navigation
