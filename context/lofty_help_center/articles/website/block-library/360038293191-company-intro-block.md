## Introduction

The "Company Intro Block" is a quick reference block that can be created to display team performance details as well as a description of the team/company. This is a useful place to feature a brand name and what is offered to clients.

## Summary

- Adding Company Intro Block

## Adding Company Intro Block

Click “Blocks” and the block library will appear.

Find the Company Intro block in the block library. Choose a style that you want to use and drag it to the page on the right side of the CMS Tool:

Double-click the Company Intro block or click the "Edit" icon at the top right of that specific block, and all content that can be edited for this block will be displayed.

Could you configure your Company information, performance, and background? If you do not fill in an item, it will not be displayed on the website.

After completing the rest of the configuration, don't forget to click the "Publish" button in the top-right of the CMS Tool for all changes to apply to the live website.
