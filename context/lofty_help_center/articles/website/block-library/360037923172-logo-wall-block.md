## Body

## Introduction

The "Logo Wall Block" allows for you to show off successful projects, industry certifications, media features, or your preferred partner's brand. This block allows you to add a name, an image (typically the logo) and a hyperlink (normally the partner's website/signup page).

## Summary

- Adding Logo Wall Block

## Adding Logo Wall Block

Click “+ Blocks” and the block library will appear.

Find the Logo Wall block in the block library. Choose the style that you want and drag it to the right side of the CMS Tool to place it on that page and in that spot.

Double-click the Logo Wall block or click the "Edit" icon found in the top-right of that specific block, and all of the content that can be edited in this block will be displayed:

Click “Add Logo Link” and fill in the name, logo, and URL.

You can immediately see the partner you just added on the right side of the CMS Tool (preview).

After completing the rest of the configuration, don't forget to click the "Publish" button in the top-right of the CMS Tool for all changes to apply to the website.
