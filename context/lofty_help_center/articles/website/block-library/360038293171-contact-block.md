## Introduction

The "Contact Block" is a simple way to present phone, email, and office address information on specific portions of your website. Follow the steps below to add this block to the desired page.

## Adding Contact Block

Click “Blocks” and the block library will appear.

Find the contact block in the block library. Choose a style that you want to use and drag it to the page on the right side:

Double-click the contact block or click the "Edit" icon on the top-right of that specific block and all of the content that can be edited for this block will be displayed.

Configure your contact details. If you do not fill in a field, it will not be displayed on the website.

After completing the rest of the configuration, don't forget to click the "Publish" button in the top-right of the CMS Tool for all changes to be applied to the live website.
