## Body

## Introduction

Your logo represents your company's brand. It is typically displayed in the header or top area of a website and serves as a quick and recognizable identifier for visitors. Clicking the logo will bring website visitors back to your home page.

When uploading your logo to your Lofty website, we recommend using a .png file that is 96 pixels in height. Generally, a logo that is twice as wide as it is tall works best.

## Uploading a Logo

To upload your website logo, navigate to the website editor, edit the header, and click the edit icon to upload your logo.

The logo location will vary depending on the style of your header. You may add up to two (2) logos.

Scroll down to the footer to add your logo there.
