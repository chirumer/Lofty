## What are Backlinks

Backlinks are links from one website to another, acting as endorsements that affirm the credibility and relevance of your content to search engines such as Google. Backlinks are essential to SEO.

A top-tier backlink typically comes from a respected site within the same or a related industry, greatly enhancing the search engine ranking of the linked site.

The greater the number of high-quality backlinks from pertinent sources like real estate websites, news outlets, or local directories, the better your website's ranking in search results for local homebuyers and sellers. This results in heightened online visibility, drawing more qualified leads and ultimately expanding your real estate business.

## Understanding the Importance of Backlinks

Backlinks are a major piece of the SEO (search engine optimization) puzzle.  You need one to complement and elevate the other.

High-level, here are some quick reasons why it’s important to have a good backlink strategy:

1. Backlinks are how Google (and other search engines) discover your pages.
2. They demonstrate to Google that your website has a trustworthy reputation, which encourages search engines to direct users to reliable sites.
3. Relevant backlinks can enhance your website's authority within your industry or niche.
4. Backlinks drive more traffic to your website, increasing your visibility and potential customer base.

In essence, a well-executed backlink strategy not only improves your SEO but also establishes your site as a credible source of information, driving organic traffic and ultimately contributing to the growth and success of your online presence.

Here’s an example. When someone types “how to sell my house in [feature area]” or “real estate agent in [city]” into Google, you want your website to come up on the first page.

If your website does appear on the first page, you're tapping into significant website traffic, generating leads, and closing crucial deals. A staggering 93% of internet experiences start with a search engine, a trend that holds true in the real estate industry. When potential clients—your target market—are seeking assistance, they turn to Google.

Appearing on the first page of search results is paramount for business success.

## Strategies for Building Backlinks

### Building Backlinks with Local Citations

Getting listed in relevant directories is crucial for building your online presence and enhancing local SEO, not just for acquiring backlinks. Here’s why citations are essential and how to identify the best directories for your real estate business:

#### What are Citations?

Citations are online mentions of your real estate business that typically include your Name, Address, and Phone Number (NAP). These mentions can appear on various online platforms such as:

- Local Business Directories: Yelp, Google My Business, Bing Places for Business, etc.
- Industry-Specific Directories: Realtor.com, Zillow, Trulia, etc.
- Local Government Websites: City or county government websites often list licensed real estate agents.
- Chamber of Commerce Listings: Joining your local chamber can provide valuable directory listings.

#### Benefits of Citations for Real Estate Businesses:

- Improved Local Search Ranking: Search engines such as Google use consistent NAP citations (Name, Address, Phone Number) across various websites to verify the legitimacy and location of your business. Consistent and accurate citations can significantly boost your website's ranking in local search results for relevant real estate keywords.
- Increased Online Visibility: Citations help potential clients discover your real estate business when they search for agents or properties online. Listings on directories often include basic contact information, reviews, and sometimes links to your website, enhancing your visibility and directing more traffic to your site.
- Backlink Potential: Many reputable directories provide backlinks to your website along with your citation. These backlinks contribute to your overall SEO efforts, improving your website's authority and potentially increasing its ranking in search engine results pages (SERPs).

#### Finding the Right Directories:

- Focus on Relevance: Don’t just submit your website to every directory you find. Prioritize directories relevant to your location, the real estate niche you serve, and those trusted by potential clients.
- Local Directories First: Start with local business directories and industry-specific real estate platforms.
- Use Online Tools: Several online tools like Moz Local or Yext can help you find high-quality directories and manage your citations across different platforms.

#### Maintaining Accuracy:

Once you’ve claimed your listings on relevant directories, ensure your NAP information is consistent across all platforms. Any inconsistencies can negatively impact your local SEO. Regularly monitor and update your listings as needed.

By building a strong citation profile through local directories, you establish your real estate business online, improve your local search rankings, and potentially earn valuable backlinks to your website. This comprehensive approach strengthens your online presence and attracts more qualified leads.

### Link Building on Social Media

While social media links themselves do not directly impact SEO, the visibility and engagement they foster can lead to natural backlinks from various online sources.

Lofty's Social Studio offers a powerful tool to promote your real estate content and attract backlinks.

Here’s how to maximize your efforts:

1. Identify Your Target Audience: Platforms: Not all social media platforms are created equal. Focus on platforms where your target audience spends their time (e.g., Facebook groups for local residents, LinkedIn for industry professionals). Communities: Join relevant Facebook groups, online forums, and subreddits focused on real estate in your area or niche.
2. Content Strategy for Backlinks: Post Consistently: Regularly share valuable content like listing posts, blog posts, local market updates, home buying tips, or visually appealing property listings. Optimize for Sharing: Include clear calls to action, encouraging viewers to share your content. Use relevant hashtags and engaging visuals to increase share ability.
3. Engage and Build Relationships: Be a Valuable Contributor: Don’t just post your content and disappear. Participate in discussions, answer questions, and offer helpful advice. Establish yourself as a trusted resource within the community. Collaborate with Others: Look for opportunities to collaborate with other real estate agents, lenders, or local businesses on social media campaigns. Cross-promotion can expand your reach and attract backlinks from their audience.

### Consistent Content Creation

Produce detailed guides on local real estate trends and informative posts about market dynamics to attract backlinks from local news sites and community blogs.

Think of every piece of content like a door to your website. The more doors you have, the more people can visit your website, and the more opportunities you have to rank in Google. A good rule of thumb, though, is to create only one piece of content for each keyword phrase you’re targeting.

With the right strategies, your piece of content can become the show everyone’s talking about.

Informative Real Estate Guides

Informative guides are the map, making the journey easier and the destination clearer.

- Easy-to-Digest: Provide clear and concise guides that break down complex real estate topics.
- Helpful Tips: Include actionable advice for both new and seasoned property seekers or sellers.
- Up-to-date: Regularly update guides to reflect the current market trends and regulations.

Visual Content

Imagine diving into a property without even stepping in! That’s the magic of visual content in the real estate industry. It brings properties to life right on the screens of potential buyers.

- Virtual Tours: Give a 360-degree view of properties, letting viewers feel the space without a physical visit. You can create the perfect virtual tour with WPVR.
- Engaging Imagery: Use high-quality photos of properties, making them irresistible to viewers.
- Infographics: Break down real estate data into simple visual stories, making it easier to grasp.
- Video Content: Showcase properties with dynamic videos, offering viewers a cinematic experience of homes on sale.

Geo-targeted Content

Geo-targeted content in real estate is like having a local guide in a foreign city. It offers insights specific to a location, ensuring your piece of content hits home—quite literally!

- Local Market Reports: Dive into specifics about a city, suburb, or even a street. What’s hot and what’s not?
- Cultural Highlights: Showcase local festivals, traditions, or events. Give a feel of the community spirit.
- Area Profiles: Detail amenities, schools, parks, and transport links in a particular region.
- Property Spotlights: Highlight properties in specific areas, giving potential buyers or renters a focused look.

### Establishing Yourself as the Local Real Estate Expert: Building Trust and Backlinks

Becoming a local resource in the real estate market isn’t just about generating leads; it’s about building trust, establishing expertise, and attracting backlinks through valuable community engagement. By getting involved in community activities, you can increase your visibility and earn backlinks from event organizers and media outlets. Here are some strategic ways to position yourself as the go-to expert:

1. Offer Free Consultations and Resources: Free Consultations: Provide potential clients with free consultations to discuss their buying or selling needs. This allows you to showcase your knowledge, build rapport, and demonstrate your commitment to their success. Host Educational Seminars: Organize workshops or seminars on topics relevant to your target audience, such as “First-Time Homebuyer Tips” or “The Benefits of Selling Your Home in Today’s Market.” This establishes you as a thought leader and positions your website as a resource for valuable information. Create Free Downloadable Resources: Develop downloadable guides, checklists, or market reports that provide valuable insights for buyers and sellers. Make these resources freely available on your website with clear calls to action encouraging visitors to explore your services.
2. Participate in Local Events: Industry Events: Network with other professionals at real estate conferences, workshops, or association meetings. These events provide opportunities to build relationships, exchange knowledge, and potentially collaborate on future projects. Community Events: Actively participate in local community events like charity fundraisers, neighborhood clean-up initiatives, or local festivals. This demonstrates your commitment to the community and fosters positive brand recognition. Sponsor local events to gain further visibility and establish your brand as a trusted local partner.
3. Partner with Local Businesses: Strategic Collaborations: Partner with businesses that complement your real estate services, such as mortgage lenders, title companies, or home inspectors. Cross-promote each other’s services and leverage each other’s audience reach. Local Publications: Connect with local newspapers, online publications, or radio stations. Offer your expertise as a guest speaker or interviewee on topics related to the real estate market. This provides valuable exposure and potential backlinks when your website is mentioned as a resource. Offer Free Content to Local Websites: Contribute informative articles or blog posts to local publications or neighborhood websites. These guest posts establish you as an expert and can include backlinks to your website for further information.

## Conclusion

Effective backlink building is a crucial, ongoing strategy for enhancing the digital presence and SEO of real estate websites. Each strategy outlined not only broadens the reach of your website but also solidifies its reputation and authority in the real estate industry.
