## Introduction

Lofty has built-in an easy way for you to add meta tags to the <head> of your lofty site globally. You can directly add your Google meta tag or Bing meta tag here to verify website ownership, achieve monitoring, and maintain how your website is displayed in these search engine results, as well as troubleshoot any related problems.

## Add Your Meta tags to Lofty

Navigate to the CMS Tool > Settings > Tools> Header Meta Tags:

For a detailed guide on adding a Google meta tag to verify ownership of your Lofty Site, please see here: Google Meta（Search Console）
