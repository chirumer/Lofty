## Connect Aidentified to Lofty

To set up the integration between Aidentified and Lofty, please follow the steps below.

(a) Log into Aidentified at https://app.aidentified.com/

(b) Click on the "Connect" icon found on the top menu, scroll down to the “Export” section, and then click on the Lofty logo. Add your Lofty login credentials (email and password), and click on the blue "Submit" button. Please note that only one CRM can be in use at a time with Aidentified. If you would like to connect Lofty, you will have to disconnect from any already connected.

## Using the Integration

The following are the main features available with the M2 platform:

- Connect Aidentified to LinkedIn
- Aidentified to Lofty

### Connect Aidentified to LinkedIn

This feature is very powerful as it allows you to bring in your LinkedIn network and other connections to Aidentified and find out how you might connect to new prospects coming into new wealth via your network. To set this up, follow these steps:

Click on Connect > LinkedIn > Follow the indicated steps on that page.

Once you have completed the above steps, click on Relationship Intelligence on the top menu. Then find the Overview tab below the main search bar. On this page, you will see information summarizing everything from LinkedIn and other connection imports. This same import process can be repeated to update the information (duplicate data will be merged).

Track wealth events happening in and around your network with the Money in Motion Overview.

The Prospect Finder can be used to find unique prospects with possible relationship paths from your database and Aidentified’s 100B+ AI-mapped relationships. Use extensive filters to find qualified prospects:

Discover deep insights about prospects by clicking on a profile. You will see information about their career, annual salary, wealth segment, household information, interests, and more. Possible relationship paths will be highlighted so you can always have a warm introduction to a possible new client.

Geo-farm neighborhoods or manual listings, you work in and identify residents with a higher likelihood of buying or selling by searching for specific demographic details with the Map View

### Aidentified to Lofty

You can easily send a group or individual records to Lofty, by selecting the box to the left of the prospect name and selecting “Add to Lofty”.

You can also find the Lofty integration button within profiles.

## Email Alert

Money in Motion will trigger an email alert that is sent out every 2-3 days with information that might be useful to review. It's called the "M2 Wealth Alert." It includes categories such as the following:

- Capital Raised
- Shares Sold
- Events by State

The email is sent from Money In Motion Wealth Alert <hello@moneyinmotion.com>

## FAQs

### Can users push an unlimited amount of leads from Aidentified to Lofty?

Users are allotted 2,400 credits annually. Users can use these credits as they please, and if needed, they may upgrade their account for more credits.

### Is it possible to do a bulk push of data from Aidentified to Lofty?

Yes, users can push an unlimited amount of records from Aidentified to Lofty.

### Does the data from Aidentified go through any comparison to a DNC list? Are there any restrictions on reaching out to leads found in Aidentified?

Aidentified does not currently scrub leads and compare them to a DNC list. This is the responsibility of each Aidentified user to make sure they are abiding by all U.S. and state regulations and are tactful and professional in how they reach out to individuals.
