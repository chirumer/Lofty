## Introduction

This article will review how to set up email parsing in Lofty for Spacio leads.

## What kind of Email will be Parsed?

Lofty is able to parse a new lead into your CRM based on an email alert that comes from Spacio to your Lofty-integrated email. Here are the common items that Lofty searches for in Email Parsing:

From Address: hello@spac.io Subject: Lead for xxx (Your Open House Name) Email Body:

## How to Set it Up

Login to your Bold Leads account here: https://spac.io/dashboard/

Click Settings > Integrations and then Click the ADWERX (Currently Lofty is not supported, however, this can be realized by other product integration).

Enter your Lofty-integrated email address in the Email field and then click APPLY .

Note: You can test this by registering a new lead on your open house in Spacio. The fields parsed from Spacio are Full Name, Email, and Phone. Any other fields will be parsed as Note in Lofty.

### How to find your unique Lofty email address in Lofty?

Go to the Settings page, and under the Features section on the right, click Integration to Integration page and then click Lead Capture .

On the bottom of this page, you will see Auto-import Lead via third-party providers. Here, you will see your Lofty email, which you will use to auto-import leads from Realtor.com. In this section, click Copy .
