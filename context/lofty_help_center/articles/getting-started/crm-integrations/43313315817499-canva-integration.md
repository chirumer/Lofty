## Body

## Introduction

Note: This feature is expected to be available soon!

The Lofty × Canva Integration allows you to create high-quality marketing materials faster and more accurately, without manual data entry. With this integration, you can:

- Access your Lofty property listings and agent profiles directly inside Canva.
- Drag and drop real-time Lofty data, including text and photos, into any Canva design.
- Export completed Canva designs back into Lofty → Design Center → My Designs for easy storage and reuse.

Best of all, a paid Canva plan is not required . You can take advantage of the integration with a free Canva account .

## How to Access the Integration

### Step 1: Install the Lofty App in Canva

1. Open the Canva App Marketplace .
2. Search for “Lofty” .
3. Click Install and follow the prompts to connect your Lofty account.

### Step 2: Open the Lofty App in the Canva Editor

1. Open any Canva design.
2. Click Apps in the left sidebar.
3. Select Lofty to open the Listings, Agents, and Export tabs.

## How to Use the Integration

### Importing Lofty Listings into Canva

1. Open the Listings tab.
2. Search or browse your listings.
3. Click any listing to view its available details.
4. Drag text or photos into your design.

### Importing Agent Information into Canva

1. Open the Agents tab.
2. Search or browse the agents you have permission to view.
3. Drag any detail you need directly onto your canvas.

#### Exporting Canva Designs Back to Lofty

1. Open the Export tab.
2. Optionally rename the design.
3. Click Export to Lofty .

Your design will appear in: Lofty → Design Center → My Designs → Canva Designs
