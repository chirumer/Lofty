## Introduction

The Google Calendar integration allows for appointments to be synced to your Google Calendar from Lofty. In Lofty, there is no calendar view for appointments so an integration with Google Calendar will allow for you to align everything with your other appointments on your existing calendar.

*IMPORTANT :

- Only APPOINTMENTS are sent from Lofty to Google Calendar, not tasks. Review the Appointments Help Center article to learn more about how to use the integration.
- Changes on Google Calendar will not be synchronized from Google Calendar to Lofty

## Summary

- Setup
- Disconnect Google Calendar

## Setup

Navigate to Settings > Integrations > Google Calendar > Connect :

Select the Google account that you would like to synchronize with Lofty:

Review permissions and then click on "Allow" for Lofty to connect to Google Calendar:

Once connected, you will see Google Calendar in the Existing Integrations section:

## Disconnect Google Calendar

To disconnect Google Calendar, navigate to Settings > Integrations > Google Calendar > Disconnect :

Confirm that you would like to disconnect by clicking "OK":

Google Calendar has now been disconnected.

## FAQ

Can an agent be logged into two calendars on one account by chance?

> Users can sync to two different calendar providers at the same time.
