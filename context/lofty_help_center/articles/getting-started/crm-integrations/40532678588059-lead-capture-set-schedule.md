## Introduction

This article will review how to set up email parsing in Lofty for Set Schedule leads.

## What kind of Email will be Parsed?

Lofty is able to parse a new lead into your CRM based on an email alert that comes from Set Schedule to your Lofty-integrated email. Here are the common items that Lofty searches for in Email Parsing:

From Address: dispatch@setschedule.com Subject: NEW LEAD - xxx(lead name) - xxx(address) Email Body:

## How to Set it Up

Go to your website admin login page. Enter your Lofty-integrated email address to receive lead notifications in your Set Schedule settings, and ensure that the Send Notifications option is checked.

Note: The fields parsed from SmartZip are Full Name, Email, and Phone. Any other fields will be parsed as Notes in Lofty.
## How do you find your unique Lofty email address?

Go to the Settings page, and under the Features section on the right of the page, click Integration to Integration page and then click Lead Capture .

You will see Auto-import Lead via third-party providers at the bottom of this page. Here, you will see your Lofty email, which you will use to auto-import leads from Realtor.com. In this section, click Copy .
