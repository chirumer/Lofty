## Introduction

On occasion, it may be necessary to move from one Lofty account to another Lofty account. Obviously, when doing so, the goal is to transfer as much information as possible. Unfortunately, some data will be lost when making this transition. This article will outline what can and cannot be transferred.

- What Cannot Be Transferred

- Permissions

### What Can Be Transferred

Most features of your Lofty account can be transferred. A condensed list of transferrable data is listed below:

- The Lofty user leads with the following information: The lead's name and contact information (including physical addresses) Family member information The lead's pipeline stage The lead's organizational segment(s) Tags applied to the lead The lead's source The lead's registration date The lead's birthday All notes on the lead's profile The lead's buying/selling timeline Property Alerts, Market Snapshots, and Market Reports Any tasks and appointments The lead's search criteria and saved properties AI Assistant Status All custom data fields added in the CRM
- Partial leads
- Smart Plan Templates
- Email Templates
- Automated Email Templates
- Organizational tools Pipeline Segments Sources Tags
- The lead's Unsubscribed Status
- The Lofty user's Virtual Number
- AI Chatbot Settings and the AI Assistant's Virtual Number
- Lofty user's profile, preferences, and notifications preferences
- The Lofty user's Team Member Goals
- Transactions If there are transactions that are associated with the transferred leads and the requesting Lofty user is the transaction owner, then these transactions will also be transferred to the new account.
- Transaction Checklist Templates
- AI Assistant package, usage, settings, active conversations, and tags
- The Lofty user's website
- Lead Pond If the entire account is transferred, the lead pond can be transferred. If a single user is transferring, the lead pond cannot be transferred.

### What Cannot Be Transferred?

If you are on an Enterprise Lofty account, we cannot transfer your entire account to a Team-level account. If a single user is transferring from an Enterprise Lofty account to a Team Lofty account, any Group-related items cannot be transferred.

Some data cannot be transferred from one Lofty account to another. Below is a complete list of what cannot be transferred, organized by Lofty CRM page:

- From the People Page: Deleted Leads Column Selections and Order

- From the Lead Profile: Social Network Information Lead Score Lender Assignee Market Reports Seller Reports Home Reports Paused Smart Plans Completed and Terminated Smart Plans Lead ID and URL

- From Transaction Management: Unclaimed MLS Transactions Customized Transaction Columns Active Transaction Checklists

- From the Tasks Page: Show/Hide Selections on the Calendar My Calendar Settings Meeting Link Check for Conflicts Setting

- Listing Discovery Page: My Listings Hot Sheets

- Campaigns Page: Smart Plan Report Paused Smart Plans Specific AI Assistant Dialogue Path Active Lead Gen Billing Active Ad Campaigns Remaining Ad Budget Lead Gen Reporting Active Mailer Billing One-Time Direct Mail Campaigns Smart Plan Direct Mail Campaigns Recurring Direct Mail Campaigns Direct Mail Campaign Reporting Active, Pending, and Completed Listing Promotions Design Center Private and Shared Folders and Designs Design Center Uploaded/Recent Images, Icons, and Logos Design Center Brand Kit Landing Page Domain/Site Landing Page Auto-Creation Settings Existing Landing Pages and their Individual Configurations Social Studio Channels Existing Open House Forms Open House Questionnaire Customizations Existing Text Codes Record of Text Codes Captured Leads

- From the Reporting Page: Previous and Current Year Agent and Email Accountability Data' Previous and Current Year Business Summary Data Previous and Current Year Performance by Agent Data Previous and Current Year Site Traffic Data Existing Source Reports

- From the Settings Page: Agent Permissions Renamed and Custom Agent Roles Lender Partners Previous and Current Year Reporting Setting Goals Lead Import History Email Templates Report Unassigned Dialer Licenses Company Number Settings Notification Settings Referral Program Link

- From the Call and Chat Panel: Today's Scheduled Tasks Overdue Scheduled Tasks Active Call Lists Call History Archived Call Lists Business Hours Call List Settings AI Assistant Following Chat/Text History

### Important Definitions

Source Account

- This refers to the account that a Lofty user's data is being transferred from

Destination Account

- This refers to the account that a Lofty user's data is being transferred to

Account Owner

- The Account Owner is the individual who signed the financial agreement with Lofty for an account. They are the owners of the data in their account, regardless of the Lofty account type. The Account Owner is referenced in this process when talking about the Source Account and the Destination Account ownership

Account Status

- The Source Account must still be active for a Lofty user to be transferred from it. The Destination Account must already be set up to receive the transferred Lofty user data. A transfer of data cannot occur if the Source Account is no longer accessible or the Destination Account does not exist.

### Permissions

Approval must be obtained from the source and destination account owners in order to request a transfer from a Source Account to a new account on Lofty. Valid approval consists of the Account Owner(s) being copied in the original request sent via email and responding in that thread indicating that they approve of the transfer. Alternatively, the Lofty Support Team can email them directly to obtain permission.
