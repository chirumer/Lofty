## Introduction

Luxury Presence is a premier website provider trusted by top-performing real estate professionals—and now, it integrates directly with Lofty. With this powerful connection, leads and activity from your Luxury Presence website sync automatically and in real time into your Lofty CRM. That means no more manual data entry, no need for third-party tools like Zapier, and no delays in following up with your hottest leads. It’s a smarter, faster way to manage your pipeline and grow your business.

To learn more about how this integration works and how to get started, visit the Luxury Presence Help Center.
