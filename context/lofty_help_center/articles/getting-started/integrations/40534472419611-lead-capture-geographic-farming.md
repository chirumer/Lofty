## Introduction

In this article, we will review how to set up email parsing in Lofty for Geographic Farming leads.

## Summary

- What kind of Email will be parsed?
- How to Set it Up
- How to find your unique Lofty email address in the CRM.

## What kind of Email will be parsed?

Lofty can parse a new lead into your CRM based on an email alert that comes from Geographic Farming to your Lofty-integrated email. Here are the common items that Lofty searches for in Email Parsing:

From Address : support@ geographic farmvalues.com Subject : New Home Valuation Lead from xxx via Geographic Farm Capture Page Email Body :

## How to Set it Up

Go to the login page for your Geographic Farming landing page. Once logged in, click on Site Editor .

Scroll to the bottom of that page to the Communication Preferences section. Then, paste your integrated email address or your unique @lofty.com address into the Also Send Emails to section.

Go to your Home Valuation landing page and submit a fake test lead to make sure the connection is working.

Note: The fields parsed from Geographic Farming are Full Name, Email, and Phone. Any other fields will be parsed as a Note in Lofty.

## How to find your unique Lofty email address in the CRM.

Go to the Settings page, and under the Features section on the right of the page, click Integration to Integration page and then click Lead Capture .

You will see Auto-import Lead via third-party providers at the bottom of this page. Here, you will see your Lofty email, which you will use to auto-import leads from Realtor.com. In this section, click Copy .
