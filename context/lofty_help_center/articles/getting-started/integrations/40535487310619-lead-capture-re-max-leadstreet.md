## Body

## Introduction

In this article, we will review how to set up email parsing in Lofty for RE/MAX LeadStreet leads.

## What kind of Email will be Parsed?

Lofty is able to parse a new lead into your CRM based on an email alert that comes from RE/MAX LeadStreet to your Lofty-integrated email. Here are the common items that Lofty searches for in Email Parsing:

From Address : website@reliancenetwork.com Subject : RE/MAX LeadStreet Lead (xxx) Email Body :

## How to Set it Up

Once logged in to the User’s Account, select the Home tab, then choose Max Center .  From within the Max Center, you can then select LeadStreet.

Once under
LeadStreet
- choose the User’s settings.
Once within LeadStreet, you can select My Account > Account Settings . Put in your integrated email (in Lofty) in the Email Address or Alternate Email Address option and click Save .

Note: The fields parsed from RE/MAX LeadStreet are Full Name, Email, and Phone. Any other fields will be parsed as Notes in Lofty.
## How to find your unique Lofty email address in Lofty?

Go to the Settings page, and under the Features section on the right, click Integration to Integration page and then click Lead Capture .

On the bottom of this page, you will see Auto-import Lead via third-party providers. Here, you will see your Lofty email, which you will use to auto-import leads from Realtor.com. In this section, click Copy .
