## Introduction

In this article, we will review how to set up email parsing in Lofty for Home Value leads.

## What kind of Email will be Parsed?

Lofty is able to parse a new lead into your CRM based on an email alert that comes from Home Value to your Lofty-integrated email. Here are the common items that Lofty searches for in Email Parsing:

From Address : noreply@homevalueleads.com Subject :  New Lead from Home Value Leads Email Body :

## How to Set it Up

Login to your Home Value Leads accounts at https://app.homevalueleads.com/login.

Once logged in, go to Site Settings > Notification Settings > Enter your integrated email address (in Lofty) in the Send New Lead Notifications box. Don't forget to save the settings!

Note: The fields parsed from Home Value are Full Name, Email, and Phone. Any other fields will be parsed as Notes in Lofty.
## How do you find your unique Lofty email address?

Go to the Settings page, and under the Features section on the right of the page, click Integration to Integration page and then click Lead Capture .

You will see Auto-import Lead via third-party providers at the bottom of this page. Here, you will see your Lofty email, which you will use to auto-import leads from Realtor.com. In this section, click Copy .
