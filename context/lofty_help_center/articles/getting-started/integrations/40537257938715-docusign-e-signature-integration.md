## Introduction

E-signature solutions like Docusign make it easy to prepare, send, and sign documents online. Instead of relying on paper and pen, you can complete agreements securely from any device, with a full digital record of every step. This guide will walk you through the basics of using DocuSign so you can streamline your workflow and keep transactions moving smoothly.

## Integrate with Docusign

To get started, you must integrate your existing DocuSign account with your Lofty account.

Important things to note:

- Each Lofty user will need to connect their own DocuSign account.
- Each DocuSign account can only be connected to one (1) Lofty account. This means that if a team utilizes the same DocuSign login credentials, only one member of the team can integrate DocuSign. If another team member integrates the same DocuSign account, using the same login credentials, the original DocuSign integration will be disconnected. If your DocuSign integration becomes disconnected, you will lose the progress display in Lofty, but your Documents will still exist in DocuSign. For a DocuSign process created for a specific lead, only the user who created it can view it, and other users who have access to the lead cannot view the DocuSign process. DocuSign supports the following document types: DOCUMENT: .doc, .docm,.docx, .dot, .dotm, .dotx, .htm, .html, .msg, .pdf, .rtf, txt, .wpd, .xhtml, .xps. IMAGE: bmp, .gif, .heic, jpg, jpeg, png, tif, tiff PRESENTATION: pot,potx,.pps,.ppt,.pptm,.pptx SPREADSHEET: .csv, .xls, .xlsm, .xlsx

There are two (2) ways to connect your DocuSign account:

#### Entrance 1: My User Settings → Integrations

#### Entrance 2: Marketplace → Back Office Management

This integration also adds notifications and Lead Timeline records.

Notifications:

Lead Timeline and Transaction History Records:

Scenario | Timeline & History
When a DocuSign process is started | #OperatorName# sent a DocuSign envelope (#EnvelopeSubject#) to #RecipientName#.
When someone signs your DocuSign process | #SignerName# signed your DocuSign envelope (#EnvelopeSubject#).
When a DocuSign process is completed | Your DocuSign envelope (#EnvelopeSubject) to #RecipientName has been completed.

## Ways to Start a DocuSign

There are multiple places where you can start a DocuSign send.

#### Transaction Detail Page→「...」icon

#### Transaction Detail Page→ Document Tab

You can either start a DocuSign with the current document you have open or upload a new document for signing.

#### Lead Detail Page→ [...] icon

#### Lead Detail Page→ Transactions & Docs Tab

You can start a DocuSign with an existing document or upload a new one directly to DocuSign.

## Send Documents to Docusign

#### Step 1: Add Document(s) to the envelope

You can add multiple documents to the envelope. They will form a pdf, which you can edit in DocuSign and add signature information.

#### Step 2: Select your Recipients

If you are starting in a Transaction, the transaction owner, transaction contacts, lead, and lead's family members will automatically be listed as suggestions. You can also use the search bar to add any lead, team member, or vendor.

If the person is not listed or does not exist in Lofty, you can add them to the DocuSign.

#### Step 3: Add Message

The final step that takes place in Lofty is adding a message to your DocuSign. Once you have completed these setup steps, you will be taken into DocuSign to set up the signature fields on the document.

Step 4: Edit in DocuSign

After you have added your recipients and clicked Confirm, the process will automatically continue in Docusign where you can add in your signature fields and send. If you need to edit the document in the future, it will have to be done in DocuSign.

## View or Manage Your Progress in Lofty

After you send a DocuSign document, you can view the status of the document in the Transaction or lead (wherever you started).

- If you started the DocuSign in a Transaction, it will not be visible on the lead's profile. If you started the DocuSign on the lead's profile, it will not be visible on the Transaction. They are independent.

Status | Description
Draft | An envelope that you created and saved without sending. Draft envelopes will be removed from your DocuSign account after 30 days if you don't send them .
Declined | The envelope has been declined by the recipients.
Delivered | The envelope has been delivered to the recipients.
Completed | All recipients have completed their signatures.
Sent | The envelope was sent to the recipients successfully.
Signed | The envelope has been signed by the recipients
Voided | The sender can choose to cancel the envelope before completed. Recipients cannot view or sign the document once it's voided.
