## Body

## Introduction

You can synchronize events from Calendly to Lofty's Calendar.

## Setup

1. Navigate to Marketplace > Partner Products > Productivity > Connect :

or you can go to Settings> Integrations> Calendly> Connect:

Sign in or select the account that you are going to connect to Lofty:

After you sign in, the integration should be connected automatically and appear under the Existing Integrations section at the top of the page. To disconnect, navigate to Settings > Integrations > Calendly> Disconnect :
