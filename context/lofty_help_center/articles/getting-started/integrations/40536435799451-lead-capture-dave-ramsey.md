## Introduction

In this article, we will review how to set up email parsing in Lofty for Dave Ramsey leads.

## What kind of Email will be Parsed?

Lofty is able to parse a new lead into your CRM based on an email alert that comes from Dave Ramsey to your Lofty-integrated email. Here are the common items that Lofty searches for in Email Parsing:

From Address : elp.support@daveramsey.com Subject : New ELP Lead Email Body :

Note: There are two lead notification emails in Dave Ramsey. When you receive the first lead notification email (shown below), you will still need to click the link in the email they send you to get the lead information (the second lead notification) which will then be sent to Lofty.

## How to Set it Up

Enter your Lofty-integrated email address as the email that receives lead notifications in your Dave Ramsey settings.

Note: The fields parsed from Dave Ramsey are Full Name, Email, and Phone. Any other fields will be parsed as Notes in Lofty.
## How to find your unique Lofty email address in Lofty?

Go to the Settings page, and under the Features section on the right of the page, click Integration to Integration page and then click Lead Capture .

On the bottom of this page, you will see Auto-import Lead via third-party providers. Here, you will see your Lofty email, which you will use to auto-import leads from Realtor.com. In this section, click Copy .
