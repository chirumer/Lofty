## Introduction

In this article, we will review the steps necessary to integrate Lofty with Realty.com. This will allow Realty.com leads to be sent directly to Lofty the moment they are generated on the Realty.com platform.

## Locating Your API Key

1. Log in to your Lofty account.
2. Select “Settings” in the menu bar.
3. Select “Integrations” on the left-hand menu bar.
4. Scroll to the very bottom & “Copy” the API Key
5. Once you have the Lofty API Key, go to your Realty.com portal located at: https://portal.realty.com.
6. Once logged in, find Integrations in the menu on the left. Expand and select All Integrations.
7. Select Lofty from the list of possible CRMs.
8. Paste the API Key that you got from your Lofty CRM, and paste it in the empty field. Then click 'Add Integration'.

9. You may receive a final pop-up asking to send test leads. You can verify that your CRM is connected by sending the test leads from your Realty.com portal and verifying they have arrived in your Lofty CRM.
