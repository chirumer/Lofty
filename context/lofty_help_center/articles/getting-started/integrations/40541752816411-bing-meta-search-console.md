## Introduction

Bing Search Console is a free service provided by Bing that helps you monitor and maintain how your website displays in Bing search results. It can also help you troubleshoot any related problems. Lofty has built-in a direct way to help you verify ownership of your Lofty site directly in the CMS Tool.

## Set up Bing Search Console Account

The first step is to make sure you have added your website on your Bing Search Console account. More information on this topic can be found in this related article written by Bing: Add & Verify your website .

## Verify Ownership of your Lofty Site

First, navigate to the CMS Tool > Settings > Integrations > Header Meta Tags:

From the Bing Webmaster Tools account, copy your verification string and paste it to CMS.

Be sure to click on the blue "Save" button and this will begin working almost immediately.

More information on how to find the verification string can be found in the following article by Bing: Verify Ownership of Your Website .
