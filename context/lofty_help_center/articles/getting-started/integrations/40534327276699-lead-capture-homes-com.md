## Introduction

In this article, we will review how to set up email parsing in Lofty for Homes.com leads.

## What kind of Email will be Parsed?

Lofty is able to parse a new lead into your CRM based on an email alert that comes from Homes.com to your Lofty-integrated email. Here are the common items that Lofty searches for in Email Parsing:

From address : noreply@homesconnect.com

## How to Set it Up

Login to your Homes.com account. Once logged in, go to Update Your Profile.

Go to the Contact Info Tab and add your integrated email address in the box labeled Email , then hit Save and Close .

Note: The fields parsed from Homes.com are Full Name, Email, and Phone. Any other fields will be parsed as Notes in Lofty.

## How to find your unique Lofty email address in Lofty?

Go to the Settings page, and under the Features section on the right, click Integration to Integration page and then click Lead Capture .

On the bottom of this page, you will see Auto-import Lead via third-party providers. Here, you will see your Lofty email, which you will use to auto-import leads from Realtor.com. In this section, click Copy .
