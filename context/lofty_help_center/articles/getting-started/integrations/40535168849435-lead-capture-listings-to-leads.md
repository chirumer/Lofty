## Introduction

This article will review how to set up email parsing in Lofty for Listings to Leads.

## What kind of Email will be Parsed?

Lofty is able to parse a new lead into your CRM based on an email alert that comes from Listings to Leads to your Lofty-integrated email. Here are the common items that Lofty searches for in Email Parsing:

Lead Notification 1 -

From Address : donotreply@listingstoleads.com Subject : Another Listings-to-Leads LEAD: Buyer Lead for xxx(address) Email Body :

Lead Notification 2 -

From Address : donotreply@listingstoleads.com Subject : Request to know about Coming Soon home: Call For Address, xxx (City) Email Body :

## How to Set it Up

Login to Listings To Leads homepage: https://www.listingstoleads.com/

Once logged in, click Profile . On the Profile page, scroll down to the Contact Details section and input your integrated email address (in Lofty) in the "Alternate Email" field (see screenshot below).

In the Send Leads To column, select the checkbox next to the Alternate Email field where your email address is entered. Scroll to the bottom of your Profile page and click Update & Save .

Note: The fields parsed from Listings to Leads are Full Name, Email, and Phone. Any other fields will be parsed as Note in Lofty.
## How to find your unique Lofty email address in Lofty?

Go to the Settings page, and under the Features section on the right, click Integration to Integration page and then click Lead Capture .

On the bottom of this page, you will see Auto-import Lead via third-party providers. Here, you will see your Lofty email, which you will use to auto-import leads from Realtor.com. In this section, click Copy .
