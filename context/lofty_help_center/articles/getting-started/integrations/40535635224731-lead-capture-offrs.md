## Introduction

In this article, we will review how to set up email parsing in Lofty for Offrs leads.

## What kind of Email will be Parsed?

Lofty is able to parse a new lead into your CRM based on an email alert that comes from Offrs to your Lofty-integrated email. Here are the common items that Lofty searches for in Email Parsing:

From Address : raia@offrs.com Subject : Lead Sent to Email Email Body :

## How to Set it Up

Enter your Lofty integrated email address as the email that receives lead notifications in your Offrs settings.

Note: The fields parsed from Offers are Full Name, Email, and Phone. Any other fields be parsed as Notes inLofty.
## How to find your unique Lofty email address in Lofty?

Go to the Settings page, and under the Features section on the right of the page, click Integration to Integration page and then click Lead Capture .

On the bottom of this page, you will see Auto-import Lead via third-party providers. Here, you will see your Lofty email, which you will use to auto-import leads from Realtor.com. In this section, click Copy .
