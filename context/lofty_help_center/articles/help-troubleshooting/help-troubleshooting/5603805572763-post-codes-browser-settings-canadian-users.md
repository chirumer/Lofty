# Post Codes Browser Settings - Canadian Users

## Body

## Introduction

Due to browser settings in Chrome, some of our Canadian users may have issues inputting their correct street addresses due to Post Codes. This can be solved by adjusting settings in your Chrome Browser.

## Usage

In Chrome-

Go to Settings > Advanced > Language > Language > English (Canada) > Move the option to the top.
