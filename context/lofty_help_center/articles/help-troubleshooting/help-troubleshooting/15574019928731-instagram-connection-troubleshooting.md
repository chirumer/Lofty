## Important

Please note the following key points:

- Your Instagram account must be a Business account and it must be connected to a Facebook Page.
- Your Facebook Profile must be an Admin for that Facebook Page.
- If you have a Creator account, you will not be able to connect your Instagram to Social Studio. Instagram only supports publishing to business accounts at this time.

## How to identify your Instagram Account Type

Unsure what type of Instagram account you have? Follow these steps to find out!

1. Open the Instagram app on your mobile device and go to your profile.
2. Tap on the hamburger menu (three horizontal lines) in the upper right corner of the app and select Settings and Privacy.
3. Select Account Type and Tools. Here, you will see the following options, depending on your current account type:

- If you currently have an Instagram personal profile, you'll see these two options: Switch to Professional account Add new Professional account
- If you currently have an Instagram business account, you'll see these two options: Switch to Personal Account Switch to Creator Account
- If you currently have an Instagram creator account, you'll see these two options: Switch to Personal Account Switch to Business Account

## Convert to Instagram Business Account

### Via Instagram

1. Open the Instagram app on your mobile device and navigate to Settings and Privacy.
2. Select Account Type and Tools.
3. Tap Switch to Professional account.
4. Follow the on-screen prompts, ensuring you choose the business account type , not the creator account type. You'll also need to select a Facebook Page (or create a new one) to link your Instagram business account to. You will see a "Don't Connect to Facebook Now" option, but if you don't connect a Facebook Page to your Instagram account, you won't be able to connect your account to Lofty Social Studio.
5. Your Instagram account should now have been converted to a business account. https://business.instagram.com/getting-started

### Via Facebook

1. Head over to Facebook and navigate to the list of Pages you have access to (or click this link ). Click on the Page that you'll associate with your Instagram account, or create a new Page if you don't already have one.
2. Once you're on your Page, click Settings in the left-hand sidebar.
3. Click Linked Account→Instagram on the left-hand side and then click Connect Account towards the top of the screen.
4. Enter your Instagram login credentials and click Log In. (If you're already signed into Instagram, or your login credentials are saved within your browser, you'll just need to click Continue.)
5. You'll see a pop-up window prompting you to set up your Instagram business account. Follow the on-screen instructions, be sure to enter your contact info, and then click Done.

### Troubleshooting Checklist

1. Head over to Facebook and navigate to the list of Pages you have access to (or click this link ). Click on the Page that is linked to your Instagram business account.
2. Once you're on your Page, switch to the page account and click Settings in the left-hand sidebar.
3. Once in settings, you should see your Linked Account connected to the Page. You should also see a section that says "Business contact information". In this case, please continue to the next step.
4. If you see a screen that differs from the image above, it means your Instagram account is either not connected to your Facebook Page, or it hasn't been converted to a business account. The following outlines some of the options you might see and the steps you should take. If you see a button that says Connect Account , it means your Instagram account is not linked to your Facebook Page. If you see a button that says Get Started , it means your Instagram account is linked to your Facebook Page, but it hasn't been converted to a business account. Click Get Started and follow the on-screen prompts to convert your Instagram account to a business account. Then, try connecting your Instagram account again If you see a button that says Review Connection , it means that the account must be confirmed before third-party applications are able to verify the connection. Please click the button and follow the prompts to confirm. Note: If you encounter issues with this process, you may need to reach out to Facebook Support in order to confirm the connection.
5. Finally, we need to check that Social Studio has all of the permissions required to access your Facebook account. Click on the small drop-down arrow at the top right of your screen and then click Settings & privacy > Settings. From there, click Business Integrations on the left-hand side (or click this link ).
6. Find Social Studio in the list of integrations and click View and edit.
7. In the pop-up window, ensure that all Facebook Pages are checked in all sections. Note: If you don’t see any individual Pages listed in the following screens, it means that you’ve previously granted Social Studio permissions to access all Pages, which is the ideal setup. In this case, you won’t need to check any Pages here, and instead, you’ll need to reestablish the authorization: on the same page above, click Remove Lofty Social Studio and complete the steps on the popup window.
8. Once that's done, try connecting your Instagram account to Social Studio again.

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### Q: How can I make sure that my Facebook page is connected to an Instagram business account?

A: You will need to log in to the personal Facebook profile that has the admin role on the Facebook Page connected to your Instagram account. If you log into a Facebook profile that does not have the admin role on the Page, or if your profile isn't correctly set up as an admin within Facebook Business Suite, you will not see your Instagram business account listed in the next step. For more information on how to see your Facebook page role or access, please visit: https://www.facebook.com/help/510247025775149

#### Q: How do I connect to the Facebook page via Instagram?

A: Follow the steps below:

1. Go to your Instagram profile.
2. Select Edit Profile.
3. Under Public Business Information, select Page.
4. Tap Create Facebook Page or Connect an Existing Page.
5. Choose a Page from your Pages that you'd like to connect to or select Create a New Facebook Page.
6. Tap Done after you've selected a Page or created a new Page. For more information, please see: https://help.instagram.com/570895513091465

#### Q: What if my Facebook page is not listed?

A: First, verify that your personal Facebook profile has the admin role for the page. If you page is part of a Facebook Business Suite account, make sure that your permissions are fully set up.

1. In Facebook, open your page and check that you've logged in as your Facebook Page, not your profile.
2. Open the menu on the left and select Professional Dashboard
3. Click Page Access
4. When you select your name, a pop-up will appear. It should say "This person has full control."

Please note that you cannot connect a Facebook page if your profile is an admin via inheritance from a parent page. To check this visit your Facebook page, navigate to the Page Roles section within the settings, and look for your name under Admin (inherited from parent Page). If you see this, you will not be able to authorize the connection between the Facebook Page and Lofty.

Next, ensure that you have authorized all page permissions. If you have the correct page role, but the page is still not listed, it means that Lofty does not have the necessary permissions from Facebook. Here are the steps to check your permissions:

1. Log into the Facebook personal profile that has admin access with Full Control on the Facebook Page.
2. Click on the small drop-down arrow at the top right corner and select Settings & Privacy > Settings
3. Head to Business Integrations in Facebook. Please note that these settings are found on your Facebook profile, not within the Facebook Page settings, so you must be using Facebook as your profile to access them.
4. Find Lofty in the list and select View and Edit
5. Scroll down to the list of Pages and verify that ALL boxes are selected. You may need to click See all pages to expand the list of pages and see the ones that permissions to be granted. Here, tick all of the empty boxes.
6. After selecting all of your accounts, click Save. If you are connecting a Facebook Page , head back to Lofty and try connecting the page again. If you are connecting an Instagram account , try connecting the account to Lofty. If it still doesn't work, please try the next steps.

Checking the connection between Facebook and Instagram

Once you have verified that your Instagram account is a business or creator account, next check that your Facebook Page is properly connected to Instagram within your Page settings and Meta Business Suite.

1. Log into the Facebook Page linked to Instagram
2. Click on Professional Dashboard
3. Click Linked Accounts
4. You should see your Instagram account connected: If your Instagram account is connected properly, the options will be View Details and Disconnect Account , as shown in the screenshot above.
5. If you see a button that says Connect Account , your Instagram account is not linked to your Facebook Page
6. If you see a button that says Get Started , it means that your Instagram account is linked to your Facebook Page, but it has not been converted to a business or creator account. Click Get Started and follow the on-screen prompts to convert your Instagram account to a business account. Then, try connecting your Instagram account again.
7. If you see a button that says Review Connection , it means that the account must be confirmed before third-party applications are able to verify the connection. Please click the button and follow the prompts to confirm. Note: If you encounter issues with this process, you may need to reach out to Facebook Support in order to confirm the connection.
