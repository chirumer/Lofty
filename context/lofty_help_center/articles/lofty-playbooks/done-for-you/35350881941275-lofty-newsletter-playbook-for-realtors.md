## Body

### Why Realtors Should Send Newsletters

Sending newsletters to your contacts is an excellent way to stay top-of-mind and build long-term relationships. Here are the top reasons why realtors should consistently send newsletters:

1. Stay Connected with Past Clients – Keep your name in front of past buyers and sellers, increasing the likelihood of referrals and repeat business.
2. Demonstrate Market Expertise – Provide valuable insights into the local real estate market, positioning yourself as a go-to expert.
3. Promote Local Events & Businesses – Strengthen your community presence by sharing local happenings, restaurants, and businesses.
4. Generate Leads & Engagement – Keep potential buyers and sellers engaged with relevant content, prompting them to reach out when ready.
5. Showcase Recent Transactions – Highlight homes you've helped buy or sell to build credibility and social proof.

### Creating a Newsletter with Lofty

Lofty offers multiple ways to create and send engaging newsletters:

#### 1. Using the Design Center

- Access the Advanced Editor in the Design Center to create visually appealing newsletter templates.
- Customize layouts, images, and branding to maintain consistency across your communications.
- Once finalized, send the newsletter directly to your contact list within Lofty.

#### 2. Using the Source Code Editor

- Import templates from external sources like Mailchimp or Constant Contact using the Source Code Editor.
- Modify the HTML as needed to personalize the newsletter for your audience.
- Save templates for future use to maintain branding and streamline the process.

### Best Practices for High-Engagement Newsletters

To maximize engagement and effectiveness, consider these best practices:

- Segment Your Audience – Tailor newsletters based on client types (buyers, sellers, investors, past clients, etc.).
- Include Local Content – Share upcoming events, new eateries, or neighborhood news to add value beyond real estate.
- Use Engaging Subject Lines – Capture attention with compelling and curiosity-driven subject lines.
- Keep It Visual – Use high-quality images and a clean design to make your newsletter visually appealing.
- Call to Action (CTA) – Encourage readers to take action, such as visiting your website, scheduling a consultation, or following you on social media.

### Example of an Engaging Newsletter

A well-performing newsletter could include:

- A section highlighting clients you've helped buy/sell in the past.
- Market updates, such as changes in interest rates or new listings.
- A spotlight on local businesses, restaurants, or community events.
- A personal touch, such as a brief update on your latest activities or industry insights.

By consistently sending newsletters through Lofty, realtors can nurture relationships, establish authority, and ultimately drive more business.
