## Introduction

The Email Accountability report is designed to provide Lofty users with insight into the number of emails sent, opened, replied to, bounced, or unsubscribed. This information can be useful in determining how to utilize email effectively in Lofty. This report can be found under Reporting > Communication > Email Accountability .

*Note: All reports include both Company/Office and Personal Leads (reference Lead Privacy: Company/Team Leads vs. Private Leads ).
