## Introduction

Update: Social Studio Growth and Elite are now retired and have been replaced by AI Marketer. To see the latest Social Studio offering, please check out AI Marketer

________________________________________________________________________________________

While the Social Studio is available to all Lofty users, we offer an advanced version, allowing additional social media features. This article covers the Social Studio Growth and Social Studio Elite add-ons and the features only available in this upgraded version. For a breakdown of the standard Social Studio features, please see our Help Center article: Social Studio

## How to Purchase

The Social Studio add-ons are available in the Lofty Marketplace:

We offer two (2) versions, Social Studio Growth and Social Studio Elite.

### Social Studio Growth

- Cost: $19.99 per month
- Connect six (6) social media profiles
- Create unlimited auto-listing videos without a watermark
- Set up automatic listing posts
- Unlimited AI-generated social post captions

### Social Studio Elite

- Cost: $39.99 per month
- Connect up to eleven (11) social media profiles
- Ability to connect additional social media profiles for $2/profile per month
- Create unlimited auto-listing videos without a watermark
- Set up automatic listing posts
- Unlimited AI-generated social post captions

What is the character limit for posting, and can this be increased?

- Character limitation is from the social platform, which is 400 characters ; it is not supported to change.
