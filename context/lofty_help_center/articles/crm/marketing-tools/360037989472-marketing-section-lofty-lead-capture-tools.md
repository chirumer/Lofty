## Introduction

On the Marketing tab, you can manage all your lead capture channels available in Lofty, including Lofty Lead Generation. You can find the Landing Pages and Open House under the Content Tab, while the listing promotion tool is known as Text Codes, is under the Automation Tab.

## Sales Agent

The Sales Agent settings can be accessed via the Marketing page. Learn more about the Sales Agent here: Sales Agent/ Chatbot Qualification Process

## Automation

The Automation section includes settings for Smart Plans, Home Reports, Auto Property Alerts and Text Codes.

### Smart Plans

Smart Plans are a flexible way to stay in touch with your leads. Learn more about Smart Plans here: Managing Smart Plans

### Home Report

Home Reports are an add-on that allows you to keep homeowners up to date on the value of their home. Learn more here: Home Report

### Auto Property Alerts

Auto property alerts allow you to keep leads up to date with listings that match their search criteria. Learn more here: Setting Up and Using Auto Property Alerts

### Text Codes

A text code allows you to set up a 6 digit code that when a lead texts, they will be sent a link to a listing. This text code can be included on flyers, social media posts, and other marketing materials. Learn more here: Text Codes for Lead Capture

## Lead Generation

When you purchase lead gen services with Lofty, you can view performance and funnel conversion data for your lead gen ads via the Lead Generation section.

Learn more about Lead Generation here: Lofty Lead Generation FAQ

## Lofty Blast

### Listing Blast

The Lofty Blast section contains Listing Blast, formerly known as Listing Promotion Ads. Listing Blasts allow you to promote your listings via Facebook ads. Learn more here: Listing Blast

### Zip Code Blast

Zip Code Blast is a self-purchase lead generation option that leverages Facebook Ads to capture buyer and seller leads in a specific zip code. The AI Assistant is included for any leads generated as a result of this package.

Why is Blast awesome?

1. Cost Blast is one of our lowest-cost lead generation options. This gives you, our users, an affordable way to find new business opportunities.
2. Availability Previously, you needed a specific listing to create a Facebook ad. With Blast, you can generate an ad using a zip code you would like to target. This opens the availability to anyone, regardless of whether or not you have a listing. It also allows you to target specific areas you may specialize in, or want to specialize in.

You can pick between a one or a two-week Blast campaign.

- One week: $79
- Two weeks: $158

After the purchase charges sare uccessfully, processed a window will appear where you can customize the settings that apply to the leads captured by the ads.

After your first purchase of Zip Code Blast, you will be able to create recurring Ad campaigns.

IMPORTANT!

If you do not fill out these settings, we will use the default values. If this window is closed without being completed, you cannot go back and edit it.

## Brand Awareness

The Brand Awareness section contains Sphere Ads, Local Service Ads, and Geographic Farming. These advertising tools aim to promote you and your business in the areas you work in most often.

### Sphere Ads

Sphere Ads help you increase local awareness of your brand. Learn more here: Sphere Ads

### Local Service Ads

Local Service Ads help you connect with people in your area who are searching for the services that you provide. Learn more here: Google Local Service Ads (LSA)

### Geographic Farming

Geographic Farming helps you dominate your local markets by targeting specific neighborhoods with postcard ad campaigns. Learn more here: Geographic Farming

## Marketing

The Marketing section contains all of the other features available to help you with your marketing efforts.

### Social Studio

The Social Studio is your hub for social media marketing. Lofty offers a free social studio as well as a paid version, which includes additional optimizations. Learn more here: Social Studio

### Direct Mail

The Direct Mail is your home for physical mailers, such as letters and postcards. Learn more here: Print House: Mailers

## Content

### Landing Pages

In the Landing Pages section, you can create landing pages to capture new leads via your preferred marketing channels. You can also view your landing page performance to evaluate their performance at capturing leads. Learn more here: Landing Pages

### Design Center

The Design Center has a library of designs and allows you to create new designs to use for mailers, social media posts, or your IDX website. Learn more here: Design Center

### Open House Forms

Open House Forms are created for an open house that you are hosting to quickly and easily collect visitor information. A potential lead completes the form at the Open House, and their information is automatically imported into the CRM. Learn more here: Open House Forms
