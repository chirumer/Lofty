# AI Assistant Smart Notifications

## Body

We heard your feedback: too many notifications can be overwhelming, and it's not always clear which ones matter most. That's why we launched AI Assistant Smart Notifications to surface the most meaningful updates and help you take action faster.

#### What's different about Smart Notifications?

- Intelligent Summaries, at a Glance: Smart Reminder automatically summarizes your leads' website behavior and new email or message activity. You can instantly grasp the key details without having to manually review a ton of information.
- Actionable Suggestions: Beyond just providing information, Smart Reminder offers suggestions for your next step. This helps you quickly decide on and take the most effective follow-up action.

AI Assistant Smart Notifications will alert you to high-impact activity across your leads and website, including:

New Communications

- New emails
- New texts
- New website chats
- New opportunity notifications

High Interest Signals

- A lead views a listing three or more times
- A lead saves a listing
- A lead requests a showing from a listing page
- A lead leaves a message via your website
- A lead saves a search
- A lead uses the mortgage calculator 3+ times in one day
- A lead submits a form while logged in

Re-engagement

- A lead returns to your site after being inactive for five or more days

Seller Activity

- A lead uses “Sell My Home” or “Home Evaluation” tools (must be an existing lead in your database)

Back on Market

- A lead re-registers via a third-party source or text code

Each notification is clickable—opening a chat with AI Copilots, or you can follow up, ask questions, or take next steps right away.

Specifically on iOS, you can simply long-press a notification to expand it, view more summary details, and take quick actions directly from the lock screen. This lets you stay productive wherever you are.
