## Introduction

Lofty agents and lenders can collaborate on leads where access is given to the lender to participate. The lender is only given limited access to the lead--meaning the primary owner of the lead will always be the real estate agent/team. Below is an outline of what tools are available to the lender.

## Sharing Leads With a Connected Lender

There are two ways that a lender can be given access to a lead:

### (1) Lender Lead Routing

Navigate to Settings > Lead Routing > For Lender . Lead routing rules can be created for any lender partners in this section. For more information on how to set up lead routing rules, please see Lead Routing Rules .

When a lead is assigned to a lender via lead routing rules, they will automatically receive access in their lender-type account and will receive an email notification informing them of access being given.

Please also review the following article for related information: Lender Lead Routing Rules .

### (2) Direct Lead Assignment

An individual lead can be assigned directly to a lender on the lead's profile page. Navigate to a lead profile page > Assigned To > Lender > All available lender-type accounts associated with your team will appear as options to assign

*IMPORTANT : Please note that the lender has to be connected to your account in order for them to appear as an option for assignment via lead routing rules or direct assignment.

## Lender View

The lender will see a lead profile that looks just like what is seen on the real estate agent side:

However, there are differences between what can be done with a lender-type account and what can be done with a real estate agent-type account.

The lender-type account is also not able to see the complete history of communication with the lead or lead activity on a connected IDX website. In other words, the communication activity (calls, emails, and texts) with the lead is not visible to the lender-type account. The activities timeline visible to the lender will only contain very basic system-recorded notes.

## Collaborating / Sharing Notes

By default, when a real estate agent leaves a note on a lead profile, that note is shared with the lender. The same is the case when a lender leaves a note in the lead profile--the note is shared with the real estate agent. If you want to notify each other when adding notes, you can use the @tag option in the notes. If there is no @tag in the note, it will not trigger any email notification or in-app push to the other party.

For example, if the real estate agent wants to share a note with the lender, the agent can search for the lead profile where the lender is listed in the Assigned To section (bottom-left) and then add a note while also @tagging them:

## Tasks

Currently, tasks between a lender and an agent-type account cannot be assigned . This includes both manually-created tasks and auto-tasks via Smart Plans.

## Engagement

- Property alerts are not synced from the real estate agent side to the lender side.
- Search Criteria are synced
- Properties are also synced

## Smart Plans

Smart Plans are not synced from the real estate agent side to the lender side. A lender can only apply Smart Plans that they have built in their lender-type account and will not have access to add Smart Plans that are contained on the real estate agent side.

## Transactions

Transactions are not shared from the real estate agent side to the lender side. If a transaction is added by a real estate agent to a lead record, it will not sync to the lender view.

## Documents

Either party can upload documents and indicate whether they would like to share them with the other party.
