## Introduction

The Lofty Agent Branding Bundle is designed to help agents build and manage a strong professional brand.

We built these bundles to give agents like you a head start with Lofty. Whether you're just getting started or looking to level up your marketing, the Branding Bundles offer everything you need to:

- Launch your brand with a professional online presence
- Stay top-of-mind with clients through consistent communication
- Generate more leads with automated, high-impact marketing tools

Each bundle is thoughtfully packaged to deliver maximum value at a low monthly cost—making it easy to get started and even easier to see results.

If you’re setting up your tools for the first time, this guide walks you through configuring each feature included in the bundle.

## Setup Instructions

##### 1. Customize Your Agent Website

Your website is a vital part of your online presence. Here’s how to set it up:

1. Log in to your Lofty account.
2. Go to Website near the top-right menu of your sidebar, and in your Site List you can find your Agent Website Growth. Click on Build the Website OR, Click Settings in the top-right menu or sidebar. Navigate to Web & Domain Setup .
3. Follow the prompts to: Select a site template that fits your brand. Connect your personalized domain. Add your branding details, such as your logo, bio, and featured listings.
4. Save your changes to publish your site.

##### 2. Set Up Your Personal Virtual Phone Number

Separate business and personal communications with a dedicated virtual number:

1. Log in to your Lofty account.
2. Click Settings and then go to Call & Text under Communication .
3. Click Select A Personal Number .
4. Check that your Country Code is correct.
5. Enter your preferred area code to see available numbers.
6. Choose a number and click Save .

##### 3. Get Started with Lofty Present

Lofty Present allows you to create polished, branded Comparative Market Analyses (CMAs) to share with clients:

1. From your Lofty dashboard, go to Listings .
2. Select CMA Tools .
3. Follow the steps to generate a CMA: Add properties for comparison. Customize branding with your logo and client details.
4. Save and share your CMA with clients to provide them with valuable market insights.

##### 4. Use Social Studio for Automated Marketing

Social Studio simplifies social media marketing and helps you grow your online presence:

1. Go to Marketing in the left-hand menu.
2. Select Social Studio .
3. Connect your social media accounts (e.g., Facebook, Instagram).
4. Choose a content strategy: Use automated campaigns to promote listings or share market updates. Customize your own posts for more control.
5. Schedule or publish your content.
