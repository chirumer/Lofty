## Body

## Introduction

A collaboration tool has been added to your already powerful feature set. You will locate the My Workspace icon in the right side panel.

When clicked, you will have direct access to your message. Click the Expansion icon to open your workspace in a new tab.

## Usage

This feature currently allows the user to:

- See all your communication in one place All Chats/texts Emails Missed calls
- Assign the above to other agents on your team

On the left menu, you will see the following "folders":

- Opportunity
- Action
- Showing Assistant
- Leads Alert
- Unknown Inbound
- Task Reminder
- Listing Promotion
- Transaction
- @me

- You can PIN or delete a notification using the 3-dot option in the upper right.

## FAQs

- Unknown calls and texts will be synced to My Workspace but unknown emails currently will not be synced at this time.
- This tool is for responding to communication and not for initiating it.
