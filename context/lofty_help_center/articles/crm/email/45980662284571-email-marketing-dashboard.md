## Body

## Introduction

The Marketing → Email module gives you a centralized view of all your mass email activity. From here, you can monitor performance metrics, manage campaigns, and organize your email templates.

The module includes three main sections:

- Overview – Your email performance dashboard
- Campaigns – View and manage all mass email campaigns
- Templates – Create, organize, and share email templates

## Summary

- Email Overview Dashboard
- Campaigns
- Templates

## Email Overview Dashboard

The Overview dashboard displays consolidated performance data for all mass emails you've sent.

### Performance Metrics

You can view metrics in two ways: By Email

Metric | Description
Emails Sent | Total number of emails sent
Emails Delivered | Emails that successfully reached recipients
Emails Opened | Emails that were opened by recipients
Emails Replied To | Emails that received a reply
Emails Bounced | Emails that failed to deliver

or By Lead .

Metric | Description
Leads Sent | Total number of leads sent an email
Leads Delivered | Leads that successfully received an email
Leads Opened | Leads that opened an email
Leads Replied To | Leads that replied to an email
Leads Unsubscribed | Leads that have unsubscribed from emails

### Performance Over Time (Trend)

A line graph tracks the following metrics over time:

- Emails Delivered
- Emails Opened
- Emails Replied To
- Emails Bounced

Use this graph to identify trends and measure the effectiveness of your email outreach.

## Campaigns

The Campaigns section displays a complete list of your mass email campaigns with tools to search, filter, and manage them.

### Campaign List Features

- Campaign Name – Displays the email subject line
- Status – Shows either Scheduled or Sent
- Search – Use the search bar to quickly find campaigns
- Audience Details : If one Segment was used, the Segment name and total recipients are displayed. Click the Segment name to open the Segment page. If recipients were manually added, the label Manual Selected appears. Click it to view the recipient list within the campaign.

### Available Actions

Actions vary based on campaign status:

Status | Available Actions
Scheduled | Edit, Delete (before send time)
Sent | Preview only

### Campaign Detail Page

Click any campaign to open the Campaign Detail page, which includes two tabs:

#### Email Tab

- Preview your email in desktop or mobile mode

#### Analytics Tab (Sent campaigns only)

- View full performance metrics
- Recipient Behavior Table includes: Lead Name (click to open the Lead page) Date Opened Bounced (Yes/No) Date of Reply Quick Actions: Reply, Call, or Text
- Export – Download analytics as CSV or Excel

## Templates

The Templates section provides a redesigned template management system with folders, search, and flexible viewing options.

### Key Features

- View Options – Toggle between Card View and List View
- Folders – Organize templates into folders; All Folders is the default view
- Search – Find templates by name
- Ownership Filter – Switch between My Templates and Shared with Me

### Creating a New Template

Click Create Template and choose your editor:

- Classic Email Editor – The same editor you're familiar with
- Drag-and-Drop Email Editor – A visual editor for building emails with a back button to return to the Templates page

### Bulk Actions (List View)

When you select multiple templates in List View, the following actions become available:

- Move to Folder
- Delete
- Copy
- Cancel selection
