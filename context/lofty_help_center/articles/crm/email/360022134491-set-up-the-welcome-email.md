## Body

## Introduction

When a lead registers on a Lofty website, by default, they will receive an automated Welcome Email. This Welcome Email will provide them with their login credentials, allowing them to return to the website and access their saved searches, as well as continue browsing properties and additional content on your website. Aside from the website login credentials, you can edit the general content of this email.

## Edit the Welcome Email

The following portions of the Welcome Email can be adjusted--the rest is populated automatically using the login credentials for that lead as well as your profile information (see Complete your Lofty Profile for more information).

To edit this section, do the following:

1. Navigate to the Lofty Settings
2. Click on Communication> Email Templates on the left side
3. Select Automated Email from the four options in the middle
4. Click on the "Edit" icon on the "Buyer Lead Welcome Email" or "Seller Lead Welcome Email"

As a reminder, the headshot and contact information included in the Welcome Email to clients will be sourced from the information you have in your Profile settings. You can learn more about getting that set up in this article: Complete your Lofty Profile .

## Turn Welcome Emails On/Off

### Leads from Lofty IDX Website

The automatic trigger for the Welcome Email for leads entering from the Lofty IDX website can be adjusted directly on the same page (Settings > Communication > Email > Email Templates > Automated Emails > Toggle on/off in the "Applied" column).

### Leads from other lead sources

This type of Welcome Email is turned off by default, but the status can be adjusted by navigating to Settings > Lead Capture > Lead Capture Settings > Toggle on/off in the "Send Welcome Email?" column .

## Mass Send Welcome Emails

Lofty users can also manually send Welcome Emails for leads that are already in the database but have never received one. This option can be found under Settings > Email Templates > Automated Emails > Welcome Email . Keep in mind that this will not replace the standard process of auto-triggering a Welcome Email when a new lead enters the database.

*IMPORTANT :

- This option will only send a Welcome Email for the leads where the current user (triggering the action) is in the assigned "Agent" slot for that specific lead.

When accessing this feature, there are two scenarios to be aware of. Hovering over the "send" icon will explain:

1. "All leads have been sent login credentials." All leads in the database have received a Welcome Email at some point in time. This means that no Welcome Emails can be triggered using this feature.
2. "A Welcome Email is currently being sent for some leads. Try back later to review the status." Welcome Emails are pending delivery. Check back later to make sure all Welcome Emails have been sent to your leads.

## FAQ

### Why does the Welcome Email only show the email address and no password when I send it to a lead?

This is because the lead has already received a Welcome Email at some point in time. The username (email) and password will only be sent in the first email. If the password is forgotten, the lead can always reset the password from the website using the "Forgot?" option.

## FAQ:

Q: Why is my CRM not showing the " SEND MANUALLY " option? A: Normally, this option can be found by navigating to: Settings > Email > Automated Emails > Welcome Email:

However, you may notice that for some accounts, the “Send Manually” option does not appear:

This may be due to several factors, including:

- Opt-in requirements
- Account creation method
- Bulk sending restrictions
- User permissions
- Deliverability issues
- Custom system settings

For example, if an account has a high bounce rate, Lofty removes the option to help protect overall email deliverability. If you need to enable this option, please check the lead’s opt-in status, review the account’s compliance and email settings, and confirm that you have the required permissions.

Note: The option to resend a welcome email to individual leads will still be available directly within each lead profile:
