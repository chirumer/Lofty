## Introduction

Work with our team at Geographic Farming to set up ongoing campaigns to cultivate specific farming territories with print advertising. This article outlines what features are available in Lofty to monitor and manage a recurring postcard campaign with Geographic Farming.

To learn more about one-time postcard options, please see Self-Service Postcards .

## What is Geographic Farming?

## Sign Up with Geographic Farming

To start with a Geographic Farming postcard campaign, navigate to the Lofty Marketplace > Lofty Products > Listing and Seller Tools . Clicking on the Learn More button will send your contact details to our team at Geographic Farming who will then contact you to talk about whether this is a good fit for your marketing needs.

## Monitoring a Recurring Postcard/Geographic Farming Campaign in Lofty

If you have signed up to send postcard campaigns with Geographic Farming, you will have immediate insight into your postcard marketing directly in the Lofty platform.

Navigate to Marketing > Print House . On this page, you will have a summary of each campaign that you have coordinated with the Geographic Farming team.

Each piece of this dashboard is broken down as follows:

1. Created: Date campaign was made.
2. Status : Active : The campaign is active and moving forward. Paused : Printing has been paused per your request Completed : All deliverables have been finished and delivered Canceled : The campaign has been canceled
3. Sent : The total number of postcards that have been sent to print and deliver
4. Engaged Leads : The total number of leads that have been captured through the QR Code and Text Code engagement mechanisms (see Postcard Engagement: Text Codes and QR Codes ).

When any of the individual campaigns are clicked, you will be taken to a Campaign Details page that looks like the following:

1. A summary of the entire campaign
2. A breakdown of the individual deliverables

All campaigns that have ended will be shown as Finished:

The statuses for the individual deliverables are the following:

- Inactive
- Active
- Waiting for Design
- Waiting for Review
- Design Rejected
- Waiting for Print
- Printing
- Delivering

Each deliverable can also be clicked and you will be able to access the Deliverable Detail Page . This page is the same as what is outlined under Self-Service Postcards > Postcard Campaign Reporting .
