## Introduction

Your CRM should be an all-in-one place to organize everything for your leads and transactions. This article outlines how to upload documents to your Lofty account.

Learn more about document management here: Transaction Management Documents

## Upload a Document to a Transaction

Navigate to a transaction or a lead profile and select Documents .

Click the Add New drop down menu to add a new folder to organize your documents or a new document.

Click Upload or simply drag and drop a document to the screen to upload.

After clicking Upload , you'll see that there are many options to upload your documents to Lofty.

### File

Select file to upload a document that has been downloaded to your device.

### Email Attachment

This option allows you to forward emails attachments to Lofty and upload them has documents, using the unique email address provided here.

### Scan Docs

This option allows you to scan paper documents or upload photos to create a pdf document to upload to Lofty. This option does require that you download the Lofty Real Estate App . Select Scan Docs, open your Lofty mobile app, select the More menu, and select Scan Docs.

Then, scan the QR code here:

You can scan up to 40 pages or select up to 40 photos from the camera roll for a single document. Once the scan is complete, it will save as a pdf to your Lofty account.

### Dropbox

If you have a Dropbox account, integrate that here to upload document to Lofty directly from Dropbox.

### Google Drive

Integrate your Google account to upload files to Lofty directly from your Google Drive.

### DocuSign

Learn more about our integration with DocuSign here: Docusign Integration

## Upload a Document to a Lead Profile

Documents can also be uploaded to a lead's profile, regardless of whether they have an associated transaction or not. Navigate to the lead's profile, select Transactions & Docs , and click the + icon next to Documents.
