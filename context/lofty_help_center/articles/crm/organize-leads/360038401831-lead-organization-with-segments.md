## Introduction

The main purpose of any CRM is to provide an organization and beyond what can be done with a typical spreadsheet or contact list. In Lofty, this happens on the People Page and consists of Pipelines, Segments, Tags, as well as a variety of filters. This article will introduce Segments.

## Edit Permissions

It is important to note that only users with the Company/Team Filter permission (see Permissions ) can edit the Segments by default. All users can add Tags and use the other filters. This means that the Segments are maintained at the Team/Company or Segment level and cannot vary by users within that Company/Team or Group.

## What are segments used for in Lofty?

Segments are used for organizing and classifying your leads into different categories. Unlike pipeline stages, a lead can be categorized into multiple segments at the same time. You might consider creating segments for events, referrals, family and friends, years, specific reminder periods, etc. Segments are located in the People/Segments dropdown menu shown below.

## Active vs Static Segments

A Static Segment allows you to add leads to the segment, and those leads stay in the segment until they are removed. Further, no other leads will be added to the segment, unless they are added manually.

An Active Segment adds and removes leads from the segment depending on your selected filters. For example, if you've added a filter for a specific lead score, leads that match the lead score will be added to the segment automatically, while leads that fall out of the lead score range will be removed automatically.

## Edit Segments

Segments can also be customized to fit your workflow and team structure. The same segments will be available for the entire team and can only be edited by those with access. Click the "..." icon to edit a Segment.

There is also a "reminder" feature that allows you to set a specific reminder period for each segment, and if a lead is not contacted in that period, they will be marked as "expired." The green/red dots next to the segments indicate whether any leads have expired in that segment (meaning they have not been contacted within that period).

Hover over each segment with a reminder or check the Reminder column to see the "Expired" number and display those leads so that you can contact them. Once contacted, the reminder period will start over for that lead.

When setting the reminder period, you have the option to decide if only manual touchpoints count or if automatic touchpoints also count as a lead contact.

- When unchecked, only manual actions will count as lead contacts.
- When checked, both automatic and manual actions will count as lead contacts.

## Changing Segments for Leads

Just like with the pipeline stages, leads can be added to segments either individually or up to 200 at a time. To add individually, you can do so directly from a lead profile. Note that you can select more than one segment to be associated with the lead.

You can also add/change segments for multiple leads at once by checking the box next to the leads on the People Page and clicking the option to "Change Segments." This will add those leads to the selected segments.

## FAQ

Q: Are Segments the same as Tags?

A: Tags are generally used to classify leads that have the same characteristics. Tags are relatively simple, clear, and flexible, and are often used to group leads for easy searching. Segments are designed to group leads with similar characteristics into different categories to be used for business analysis and precision marketing.
