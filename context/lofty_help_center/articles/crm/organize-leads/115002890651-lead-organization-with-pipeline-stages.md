## Introduction

The main purpose of any CRM is to provide an organization above and beyond what can be done with a typical spreadsheet or contact list. In Lofty, this happens on the People Page and consists of Pipelines, Segments, Tags, and a variety of filters. This article will address the structure from the top level of the organization (pipeline) down to the filters that are made available.

## Edit Permissions

It is important to note that the Pipeline stages are maintained at the team level and cannot vary by user within that team. This means that the Pipeline stages are shared and visible to everyone in the Lofty account.

The ability to edit the Pipeline stages is controlled by the System Configuration > Pipeline permission toggle.

## What is a Pipeline?

Though this will likely not be a new definition for anyone with sales experience, the pipeline is the top level of organization and indicates the actual stage of the buying or selling process that a lead/client is at as you work with them.

A lead will always start at the left with "New Leads" and end at the right with "Closed."

A lead can only be in one pipeline stage at a time. This is because the pipeline stage should reflect exactly where they are in the buying/selling process.

## Customizing Pipeline Stages

The stages between "New Leads" and "Closed" can vary depending on your specific sales process.

1. These can be adjusted in Lofty by clicking on the "gear" icon to the right of the pipeline:

2. Add a new Pipeline Stage

3. Drag and drop the stages to reorder, but keep in mind that "New Leads" will always be at the front, and "Closed" should always be the finish line for a lead.

4. You can also click the "Trash" icon to delete.

*Please keep in mind that Pipelines cannot be deleted if other agents have leads in that Pipeline.

#### Example Pipeline Stages

When you first activate your Lofty account, the system will already have some default pipeline stages built in. You will want to edit these to reflect your buying/selling process.

Some recommended pipeline names that you might consider adding to your CRM (from left to right):

- Attempting Contact
- Hot
- Nurturing/Cold
- Currently Showing
- Under Contract
- Bad Leads
- Do Not Contact

All Pipeline Stages can be deleted EXCEPT:

All Leads

New Leads

Closed

All Pipeline Stages can be rearranged (by order) EXCEPT:

All Leads

New Leads

## Changing the Pipeline Stages for Leads

*NOTE: Changing the pipeline stage for leads will affect the data on the "Funnel Analysis." Reference Business Summary (Reporting) .

Pipeline stages can be changed either one at a time for individual leads or in mass by selecting them from the People Page . Here is where it is changed for one individual lead:

You can also change the pipeline stage for multiple leads at once by

(1) Check the box next to the leads on the People Page

(2) Select the option to "Change Pipeline."

(3) Select the pipeline stage where you would like to move all the selected leads. Note that this will only work for 200 leads at a time.
