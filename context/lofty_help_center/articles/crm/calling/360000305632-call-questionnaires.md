## Body

## Introduction

The questionnaire function allows you to customize and create common questions to ask your leads on phone calls. You can fill out the questionnaire during each call and have the answers to the questions saved to the lead profile.

## Summary

- Create a Questionnaire
- Use a Questionnaire
- Accessing the Questionnaire Answers

## Create a Questionnaire

Access the questionnaires settings page by clicking going to Settings > Communication > Call & Text > Questionnaires :

Click on the "+ Add" button to create a new questionnaire:

Add the "Questionnaire Name" and "Description" for future access and use:

Click on "+ Add Question" to add a new question. You can choose from three different types of questions (text box, multiple choice, or single choice). Type in the question(s) and then click on the "Create" button to continue.

Repeat the previous step to build your questionnaire. When finished, click on "Save" in the bottom-right:

Once built, you can view, edit, or delete your questionnaires:

## Use a Questionnaire

After creating the questionnaire, you can select which questionnaire to use before you begin a call list. For more information regarding call lists, please reference the following article: How To Use a Standard Call List .

The questionnaire selected will be shown to the left of the dialer panel when the lead answers the call:
