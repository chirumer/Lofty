# Sales Agent Virtual Number

## Body

## Introduction

The Sales Agent uses a dedicated virtual phone number to send texts. The Sales Agent displays a reminder when the virtual number has not been selected or cannot be used, so you can take action right away.

## How to Identify a Problem with the Sales Agent's Number

You may see an "AI virtual number unavailable" notice in the following situations:

- The virtual number has not yet been provisioned for your account
- There is an active porting or carrier registration issue with the number
- Your calling/texting package has expired or reached its usage limit
- The number is temporarily suspended due to a billing issue
- The number is not linked to a 10DLC campaign

## What the Notice Looks Like

When the AI virtual number is unavailable, you will see a yellow warning banner at the top of the Sales Agent panel:

"Your AI virtual number is currently unavailable. Sales Agent calling and texting are paused. Please contact support or check your account settings to resolve this."

## How to Resolve the Issue

Depending on the cause, take one of the following steps:

- Provisioning issue : Select a virtual phone number for the Sales Agent.
- Billing issue :Go to Billing Center → Payment Methods , update your payment information, then click the banner to restore service.
- Porting/carrier issue :Contact Lofty Support and reference your virtual number or account ID.
- 10DLC Campaign Issue : If a campaign is available, select it from the drop down menu. If there is no campaign, one must be created. Learn more here: How to Register for A2P 10DLC

## Will Sales Agent Continue Working Without a Virtual Number?

Partially. Sales Agent requires a valid virtual number to send texts on behalf of the agent. While the number is unavailable:

- Sales Agent texting will be disabled
- Email outreach through Sales Agent will continue normally (email does not require a virtual number)

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### My virtual number was working before, why is it suddenly unavailable?

The most common causes are a lapsed billing cycle, a package upgrade that requires a new number assignment, or a carrier-level registration renewal. Contact Lofty Support with your account ID and we will investigate.

#### Can my leads still reach me while the virtual number is unavailable?

Yes. Leads can still contact you through your personal agent number. Only Sales Agent's AI-initiated calls and texts are affected.

#### How long does it take to restore a virtual number?

Most provisioning and billing issues are resolved within 1 business day. Carrier-level porting issues may take 2–5 business days.
