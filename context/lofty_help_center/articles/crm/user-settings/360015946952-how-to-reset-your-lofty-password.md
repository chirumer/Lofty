# How to reset your Lofty Password

- Article ID: `360015946952`
- Category: `CRM`
- Section: `User Settings`
- Updated: `2025-11-07T06:10:09Z`

## Body

## Introduction

You can easily reset your password if you have lost access to your account for whatever reason.

Follow the steps below to do so.

Navigate to https://lofty.com/ , and click Sign In in the top-right corner.

Click on Forgot your password?

Type in the email address associated with your Lofty account and click “Send Email.”

Check your email inbox for an email that looks like the following and click the Reset Password button.

Type in a new password, confirm it, then click “Reset.”

Kindly note the password requirement:

- Must be between 9-20 characters.
- Have both letters and numbers.
- No spaces.
- The following special characters are supported: -/:;()$&@".,?!'[]{}#%^*+=_~<>¢£¥

You should see a confirmation message that looks like the following:

Navigate back to https://lofty.com/ , click on "Sign In" in the top-right corner, type in your email address and the new password, and log into the account. When a Lofty account password is changed, either by you(user) or by the Lofty support team, you will receive a confirmation email.

If you would like to change your Lofty login email address, please see Log In to your Lofty Account
