# Sunsetting of LoftyWorks (Property Management Add-On)

- Article ID: `41690402776859`
- Category: `CRM`
- Section: `LoftyWorks - Property Management`
- Updated: `2025-11-11T01:16:24Z`
- Source: https://help.lofty.com/hc/en-us/articles/41690402776859-Sunsetting-of-LoftyWorks-Property-Management-Add-On

## Body

## Introduction

We’ve made the decision to retire the current version of LoftyWorks, our property management add-on for Lofty users. This change allows us to focus on delivering the best possible experience across our core products and future innovations.

The LoftyWorks Add-On will be sunset by the end of October 2025--meaning that on November 1, 2025 you will no longer have access to this add-on.

### What is LoftyWorks?

LoftyWorks is a property management add-on built by Lofty for Lofty users that manage buyers, sellers, AND renters as property managers.

If you purchased this add-on, you would see this entrance in your top-right menu. This entrance will be removed as part of this sunsetting.

### Why is the LoftyWorks add-on being retired?

From time to time, we review our products to ensure they meet the needs of our customers and align with our long-term vision. When an add-on no longer fits our strategic roadmap, we may choose to sunset it so we can dedicate resources to building better solutions for you that matter.

### What does this mean for you?

- LoftyWorks will no longer be available after October 2025.
- LoftyWorks is no longer available for purchase as an add-on.
- Your Lofty account and other features remain unaffected.

### Can I export my data from the LoftyWorks add-on?

You can export properties, contracts, and contacts by navigating (in LoftyWorks) to Settings > Import / Export > Export .

### What’s next?

While we can’t share specific details yet, we’re working to bring an even stronger property management experience back in the future. Stay tuned for updates!

## Questions?

If you have any questions regarding this topic or any others, please email our Support Team at support@lofty.com, call 1 (855) 981-7557, or chat with us through your CRM.
