Learn more: White Label

## Enterprise Permission Improvements

#### "Manage User" Permission

The "Manage User" permission can be applied to any permission profile regardless of whether they are an admin or not. When this permission is enabled, there are two available options to limit the scope of the permission.

Depending on which one is selected, this permission gives the user the ability to. . .

- Entire Company Manage the organization's hierarchy Manage agent roles Manage permissions profiles Add/remove users and change individual user details
- User's Group Manage their current group and all subgroup agent profiles

#### Advanced Permission Settings

A new setting is available under Settings > Permission Profiles > Advanced Settings . This new setting is intended to define limitations for how organizational groups are to interact with each other within the Enterprise account. Some organizations prefer for users within these groups to only see others who belong to the same group and not users from the entire account. These permissions address that.

Learn More: Enterprise Edition - Permissions

## Lofty Workspaces: My Workspace  - Beta Feature

*NOTE* This feature is currently in Beta access and may not be open to all users at this time. Features are subject to change.

This new tool has been added to your already powerful feature set. located at the My Workplace icon on the top navigation bar. When clicked, a new tab will open in your browser. This is intended to be an immersive experience that allows you to focus on responding to communication.

With this feature, you can see and communicate all in one place. This includes chat, text, emails, and missed calls. You can also use this tool to assign these to other teammates on your platform.

Learn More: Lofty Workspaces

## AI Assistant Optimizations

### Seller Call Consultations

In order to get better engagement from seller leads, the AI Assistant will attempt to schedule a phone call meeting when the following triggers occur:

- The seller lead requests a home valuation on the associated Lofty -built website 1. The AI Assistant will ask the lead if they would like a CMA based on the property address submitted.
- Seller lead views five unique sold properties in one day If the lead has a property recorded on their lead profile (under the "Selling" label only), the AI Assistant will ask if they would like a CMA and to schedule a call. The most recent "selling" property will be referenced by the AI. If the lead does not have a property recorded on their lead profile, the AI Assistant will ask for the property address first and then offer a CMA to schedule a call.

### Compassionate Responses

With this release, the AI Assistant has been trained to respond more compassionately to messages from leads that may indicate some sort of sickness or tragedy.

### Support for Scheduling Showings via Text

This feature has already been live when the AI Assistant is engaging leads on Lofty -built websites. With this update, however, the AI Assistant will also send out text messages to leads whose behavior matches the triggers:

- Saving listings
- Sharing listings
- Viewing specific properties more than three times in a 24-hour period
- Requesting showings

Please note that these triggering behaviors do have built-in "cooling conditions:"

- The save/view/share trigger is triggered only once within 24 hours. For example, if you share a listing share the system will cool for 24 hours so if another property is shared within that period the AI will not trigger again.
- Verification if the same property is saved/viewed/shared multiple times. If there are save/view/share behaviors for the same property (that has already triggered an AI conversation) after the cool-down period, the same property needs a cool-down period of 7 days before it would trigger again.

Learn More: AI Assistant / Chatbot FAQ

## Social Studio Optimizations

#### New Post-Creation Process

Users who have not yet connected to any social channels can still see the Social Studio Dashboard and Calendar View. Previously, these were not visible. Users can also click the button to + Create a Post and they will be able to preview what the post creation process looks like and connect their social platforms directly from the new window.

#### Dashboard Redesign

The Social Studio Dashboard has been redesigned. Instead of being able to switch between the original list view and the calendar view, the default view now is the weekly calendar view and individual cards represent social posts. The weekly calendar view can be expanded to show the whole month if needed. When a user toggles between weeks on the calendar view, the cards list at the bottom will display that week's posts. Clicking on a post on the calendar takes the user to the card on the list and that card has all the associated information:

- Content
- Status
- Image
- Engagement data (leads count is clickable)
- Social channels (clickable if the post is published)
- Post date

#### Link Post Image Previews

When publishing a link to any of the supported social channels via the Lofty Social Studio, the posts will generate an image preview for that link on all platforms other than Instagram.

#### Social Feed Website Block

*NOTE : This feature is only available for users who have upgraded to Social Studio Pro .

A new block can be found in the Lofty CMS for Social Feed . This block will automatically synchronize your social posts to your Lofty -built website.

- When a site visitor clicks on any of the posts, it will redirect them to the details on that social platform.
- This Social Feed will show existing posts from connected social pages even if they were not published via the Lofty Social Studio (with the exception of LinkedIn).
- Video posts will not display
- Link-only posts will not generate a preview

Learn More: Social Studio
