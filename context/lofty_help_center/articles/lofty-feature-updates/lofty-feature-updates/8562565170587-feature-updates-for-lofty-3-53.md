Learn More: Transaction Management Introduction

## Depreciation of Microsoft Exchange Basic Authentication

Microsoft is removing support for certain Microsoft email integration methods which means that existing connections using these methods need to be updated to a supported option. We have also sent all users affected by this an email. If you need support reconnecting your email, please reach out to our Support Team

- Users who are using Outlook and integrating with Lofty using the "Exchange" or "IMAP/SMTP" option will no longer have this method supported and must reconnect.
- Users who are using a self-hosted email service and integrating with Lofty using the "Exchange" option will no longer have this method supported and must reconnect.

Learn More: IMAP/SMTP Email Integration

Learn More: Lofty Lead Generation FAQ

### MLS Compliance Updates

#### Confirmation Pop-Up for Brokerage, Logo, and Domain Changes

When you change your brokerage name, your homepage logo, or your website domain, this will typically require a review by your MLS to ensure there are accurate records and everything is compliant. Now, when these actions are taken in the Lofty CMS as you edit your website, you will see a pop-up that asks you to confirm the action. This will also trigger a ticket with the Lofty Support Team to reach out and take any additional steps if necessary.

Learn More: MLS FAQs

### AI Assistant Optimizations

The AI Assistant settings page's overall user interface has improved with this update.

The following specific optimizations have been made as well.

#### Data Report

A new Data Report shows you the following–all of which are clickable:

- Leads are currently being monitored by the AI Assistant
- Leads who have responded to the AI Assistant
- Appointments that the AI Assistant has scheduled
- Closed Transactions that the AI Assistant has been involved in

*IMPORTANT : This data is currently only available for the most recent 30 days.

#### New Engagement Options

For the engagement options of the AI Assistant, there are now only two instead of three. This is because the text message qualification settings have been combined and new options made available.

##### AI Live Chat Communication

All AI Assistant settings related to its live chat efforts on your Lofty- built website can be controlled here:

##### AI Text Message Communication

To control how the AI Assistant engages via text message with new leads entering your CRM, edit using this section:

There are now three tabs in this section, for lead types , that you can access individually. You now have more fine control over when you want the AI Assistant to engage with leads via text. You can check/uncheck the boxes and then choose when specifically it will activate for each conversation option.

### AI Assistant: Duplicate Lead Logic Improvement

Prior to this update, the AI Assistant would attempt to have conversations with all leads that have the same phone number but this would cause confusion in the conversations, logged timeline notes, and notifications. With this update, the AI Assistant will only work one lead at a time with the same phone number to avoid this issue. This simply means that if there are multiple leads with the same phone number, the AI Assistant will only have a conversation with one of them.

When you attempt to unmute the AI Assistant to get it to engage with a lead who has a duplicate phone number already qualified, you will see a pop-up informing you that the AI is already having a conversation with that phone number. This same logic applies if you have a Smart Plan scheduled to engage the AI in conversation with a lead.

NOTES:

- The above logic will apply even if there are multiple AI Assistants on one account (assigned to multiple users)
- This logic also applies even if you are on Enterprise and allow for duplicate leads to exist

### Lead Import Optimizations

The lead import process has been improved in the following ways.

##### Auto-Email Settings

Instead of having to choose how you would like to trigger auto-emails for your leads prior to processing the import, you will now be asked to do so after the import has been processed.

Learn More: Lead Import (via CSV/Spreadsheet)

##### Upload Contacts via Mobile App

You already had access to upload the leads via the Lofty Mobile App but now there is a reminder to do so from the web app and a QR code that can be scanned for easy access. When you scan the code, you will be taken directly to the installed Lofty Mobile App where you can add leads from your phone contacts.

### Onboarding Optimization

#### AI Assistant Pop-Up

If users have finished the first-time lead import process successfully but do not yet have the AI Assistant added on their account they will see a reminder to take advantage of the 30-day free trial which they can access at the click of a button. An email will also be sent based on the same criteria.

#### Lead Generation Pop-Up

For users who have finished the built-in onboarding checklist and have not yet added Lofty lead generation services to their account, they will see a reminder to take a look at Lofty lead gen. An email will also be sent based on the same criteria.

### New Website Template

*This feature is delayed till 9/8/2022*

We have added a sixth website template to our platform that is available to all users with Lofty website functionality. This style is designed to focus on luxury themes with gold and black colors.

You can select this new template by navigating to CMS > Editor > Style > Template :

*NOTE: All blocks and pages are the same as on the other templates but with the updated theme.

Learn More: Changing the Website Theme
