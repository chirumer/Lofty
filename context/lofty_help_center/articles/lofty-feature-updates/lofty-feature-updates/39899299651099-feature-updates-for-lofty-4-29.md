## AI Sales Assistant Official Relaunch - August 25th

We’re thrilled to announce the official relaunch of Sales Assistant , a massive leap forward in AI-powered lead engagement. After months of development, migration, and listening to your feedback, we’ve rebuilt our Sales Assistant from the ground up to be smarter, more human, and incredibly effective — and it’s now available to every Lofty user .

### What's New?

#### Now Powered by Leading-Edge AI Engines!

We've changed the brain behind Sales Assistant, integrating it with the world's most advanced Large Language Models (LLMS), including the latest from ChatGPT and Claude. This ensures your Sales Assistant is always operating at peak intelligence, resulting in more natural and responsive conversations that genuinely connect with your leads.

Building on the foundation you know, the new Sales Assistant is now:

Feature | Enhancement
More Flexible | Engages in fluid, dynamic conversations—no more rigid scripts.
More Human | Replies with genuine empathy to build trust and real connections.
More Intelligent | Grasps the underlying intent of a conversation, not just keywords.
More Insightful | Acts as a true expert, offering deep market knowledge and advice.
More Context-Aware | Remembers past interactions for smarter, more proactive follow-ups.

#### Speak your Client's Language: Multilingual Support!

Break down communication barriers and connect with every lead in your community. The new Sales Assistant can now automatically detect the lead's language and communicate fluently in multiple languages .

Whether your lead prefers to text in their native tongue or you're expanding into diverse markets, your AI is now your personal translator and engagement expert, ensuring no opportunity is lost in translation.

#### Industry-Leading Performance you can Measure

Don't just take our word for it. The new Sales Assistant is already delivering incredible results. With a text message reply rate of 59.4% , it significantly outperforms industry averages, ensuring more of your leads are engaged and qualified—24/7.

#### Sales Assistant for Everyone!

No more limited trial periods. We believe every agent deserves a powerful AI Partner. That's why every Lofty user now gets access to Sales Assistant with a quota of 10 leads , included with your Lofty account. It’s the perfect way to experience how AI can help you nurture leads and boost conversions from day one.

#### Enhanced Setup Experience

We’ve redesigned the setup process so you can configure Sales Assistant through a conversational interface and see it in action immediately, right from your Marketing dashboard.

#### New Overview Page

The updated Sales Assistant dashboard gives you clear insights into how AI is helping your business:

- Total Engaged : Number of leads contacted via text, chat, or email
- Total Contacted : Total outreach messages sent
- Saved Time & Cost : See how much time and money you’ve saved
- Lead Handoffs : Track when Sales Assistant flags leads for your attention
- Lead Management : View and mute leads directly from the reporting page

You can also see all leads Sales Assistant is actively working and mute them directly on this page.

## AI Smart Notifications

We're thrilled to announce the official launch of Smart Notifications , a powerful new feature that's now available to all users for free!

#### What's different about Smart Notifications?

- Intelligent Summaries, at a Glance: Smart Notifications automatically summarizes your leads' website behavior and new email or message activity. You can instantly grasp the key details without having to manually review a ton of information.
- Actionable Suggestions: Beyond just providing information, Smart Notifications offers suggestions for your next step. This helps you quickly decide on and take the most effective follow-up action.

Check out AI Smart Notifications for more information!

## AI Call Summary

Copilot can summarize key points from call recordings and suggest next steps after a call ends! This was a highly-requested feature from multiple users. This is available to all Lofty users in both the unlimited and freemium version of Copilot.

Check out AI Call Summary + Transcript for more information!

## Mobile Dashboard

The dashboard on the Lofty mobile app has been updated to more closely match the dashboard in the web app. If you've enabled notifications for the Lofty mobile app, expect to see a new notification at 8am daily with that day's top priorities.

We've also added quick actions. Swipe left to quickly text, email, or call a lead or to mark an appointment as completed or postponed.

If the daily priorities notification isn't for you, navigate to your app settings to disable this notification.

Learn more about the Lofty Mobile App here: Lofty Real Estate App

## DNC.com Integration Enhancements

If you utilize DNC.com to manage your Do Not Contact list and Business Relationships, we have an enhancement for you! This update will allow you to sync your Lofty Internal Do Not Contact list with the Internal DNC list that is hosted in your DNC.com account. We also allow you to link a specific DNC.com project to Lofty, which will help you stay organized.

Learn more about this integration here: DNC.com (Contact Center Compliance) Integration
