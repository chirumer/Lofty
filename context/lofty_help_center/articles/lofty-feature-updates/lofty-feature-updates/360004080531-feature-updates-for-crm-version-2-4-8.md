# Feature Updates for CRM Version 2.4.8

- Category: `Lofty Feature Updates`

## Body

Key Takeaways

- Ability to merge leads as duplicates or as family members(Web)
- New Transaction Management (Web)   *Only available for beta users
- Listing Management - Team/My Listings (Web) *Only available for beta users
- New Lead Routing with upgraded features (Web)
- Smart Plan - hrs/mins delay time for steps (Web)
- Export Optimization (Web)
- Ability to filter leads by whether they have email addresses or phone numbers (Web & App)
- Ability to filter leads by whether they have a Market Report set up (Web & App)
- Ability to sort leads by last call (Web)
- Ability to sort leads by last name (Web)
- Remember Last Choices of Tasks and Appointment Settings (Web)
- Opt-out leads assignment (Web & App)

Ability to merge leads as duplicates or as family members(Web)

Now you can merge 2 leads if they are duplicates or are a family. If a lead is detected as a duplicate of another lead, you will see a tip on the top of the lead’s profile page, and an option to merge these 2 leads (when you have access to both). You can also merge 2 leads by selecting both of them on the lead list page, and click ‘Merge’ in the menu.

New Transaction Management (Web)  *Only available for beta users

Transaction management is a new tab for agents to manage all their transactions in one place. In this version, we will add Transaction Status to help them keep track of their transactions and visualize transaction value.

Listing Management - Team/My Listings(Web)  *Only available for beta users

Users in Listing Management Whitelist can view their own or their team listings now. They need to enter their License ID or MLS Agent ID in Settings-Profile to get their own listings (Some MLS don’t give License ID, so we can only match listings by Agent MLS ID). Only admins can view team listings, Members can only view their own listings.

New Lead Routing with upgraded features (Web)

Here are several adjustments made in Lead Routing:

- Directly send blast emails simultaneously to all assignees in one rule
- Divide into three rules: namely, Directly assign to agent(s), Round Robin, and Blast Alert.
- Route leads to the next matched rule, instead go to the Default Rule. In other words, if the lead does not match the plan's criteria or if the lead is not engaged with then the system will revert to the next rule in the lineup
- Set a rule to be a default rule (with the same settings) for the last one listed above
- Copy a rule by simply clicking the “Copy” icon

Smart Plan - hrs/mins delay time for steps (web)

Besides the options of Immediately and Wait xx days, we add hrs/mins delay when selecting a timing for the task to execute.

Export Optimization(Web)

You can export leads with Notes, Family Members Info and Custom Field now. You can also filter out leads to export by Reg Time now.

Ability to filter leads by whether they have email addresses or phone numbers (Web & App)

A new type of filter called ‘Contact Info’ has been added to the lead list page.

Ability to filter leads by whether they have a Market Report set up (Web & App)

A new type of filter called ‘Market Report’ has been added to the lead list page.

Ability to sort leads by last call (Web)

Now you can sort leads by the time they were last called in the ‘Last Touch’ column.

Ability to sort leads by last name (Web)

Now you can sort leads by their last name.

Remember Last Choices of Tasks and Appointment Settings (Web)

For task and appointment settings on lead profile page, the system will remember last choices of the user on the same browser.

Opt-out leads assignment (Web & App)

You have the ability to manually assign opt-out leads to lenders if you are sure that the lead wants to share info with lenders.
