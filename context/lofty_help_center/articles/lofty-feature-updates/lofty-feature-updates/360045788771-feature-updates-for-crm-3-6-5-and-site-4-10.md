## Introduction

*Please note that the features available to you on the Lofty platform will vary depending on the package you have purchased. For any questions in this regard, please reach out to your assigned Customer Success Manager or our Support Team for more information.

### Release Date

The release date for this set of features is Thursday, July 9, 2020.

### Summary

- CRM Lead Import Reversal Timeframe Extension New Team Admin/Owner Lead Privacy Option Transaction Management Improvements
- Site (New CMS Only) Quick Search Block View Pattern Setting Pocket Listings and Manual Address Support Renter Lead Identification

### Lead Import Reversal Timeframe Extension

This update extends the timeframe in which a CSV lead import can be reversed from 24 hours to three days . More information on how this feature works can be found here: Lead Import: Reverse Option .

### New Team Admin/Owner Lead Privacy Option

The Team Admin/Owner can now change a lead from a team lead to a private lead without only being able to assign that lead to themselves. Prior to this update, if the Team Admin/Owner adjusted the lead privacy from team lead to private lead, the lead would be made private but assigned to them only. With this update, the lead will remain assigned to the person listed in the primary "Agent" slot in the "Assigned To" section.

This process can only be done one lead at a time, there is currently no option to do this in mass.

After changing the team lead to a private lead, the Team Admin/Owner will no longer have access to see that lead. For more information on lead privacy, see Lead Privacy: Team Leads vs. Private Leads .

A few important notes regarding transaction stages:

- In addition to "Closed" and "Cancelled," you are able to create up to 15 stages in total for each individual transaction type

### Quick Search Block View Pattern Setting

*Only available on the New CMS

When adding Quick Search Links (see Quick Search Block ), users can now select a specific "View Pattern" for each link. This will adjust how a lead views the listing pages when they click that specific quick search link, not how the quick search link displays.

### Pocket Listings and Manual Address Support

*Only available on the New CMS

This update to the pocket listings feature allows for the input of an address without having to select from the populated suggestions. This is a field that appears when creating or editing a pocket listing via the New CMS Tool > Filters > Pocket Listings . This should auto-parse into the following format: [street address], [city], [state] [zip code] using the commas and spaces. We still recommend choosing from the address suggestions if available.

### Renter Lead Identification

*Only available on the New CMS

Lofty now has the ability to automatically mark the lead type as "Renter" for new leads who register on rental listings on the IDX website.
