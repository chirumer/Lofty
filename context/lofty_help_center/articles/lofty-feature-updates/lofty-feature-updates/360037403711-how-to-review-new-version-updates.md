## Summary

Lofty releases product updates regularly to constantly improve the product. An email will be sent to the Team Owner/Admin one day before every new release.  The email will look like this:

All Lofty users will have new feature notifications in the Lofty Updates section -- as displayed on this screenshot. Question mark icon> Lofty Updates.

Click "Lofty Updates" and you can review the newest feature updates.
