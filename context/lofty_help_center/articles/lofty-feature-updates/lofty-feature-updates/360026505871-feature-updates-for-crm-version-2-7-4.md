# Feature Updates for CRM Version 2.7.4

- Article ID: `360026505871`
- Category: `Lofty Feature Updates`
- Section: `Lofty Feature Updates`
- Updated: `2025-11-12T05:22:44Z`
- Source: https://help.lofty.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026505871-Feature-Updates-for-CRM-Version-2-7-4

## Body

Key Takeaways

- Lead Routing for Custom Agent Role (Web)
- Support Intra-Communication In Lead Details Page (Web)
- Lofty APP support @ members when adding a note (App)
- Send an alert immediately after changing the alert (Web)
- Remove Beep Sound from Voice Message (Web&App)
- Change the size of the logo for automated emails (Web)

Lead Routing for Custom Agent Role (Web)

Now, in addition to Agent and Lender, the custom roles you add under Settings > Agent > Agent Role can be set to directly assign to the Team Member on the Lead Routing page. All the agent roles will be assigned simultaneously after the leads enter Lead Routing.

Support Intra-Communication In Lead Details Page (Web)

Click on the business card icon next to an assignee and a quick action popup will appear, you can call/text/email your team member from this in the lead details page. Note: all the quick actions will not be recorded on the activities timeline.

Lofty APP support @ members when adding a note (App)

You can now @ your team member when adding a note for the lead. Note: you can only @ a team member who has access to the lead.

Send an alert immediately after changing the alert (Web)

You can now choose to send an alert email immediately after you change the configuration of the buyer property alert.

Remove Beep Sound from Voice Message (Web&App)

We have optimized and removed the beep sound from dialer voice messages to make it sound more like human behavior.

Change the size of the logo for automated emails (Web)

You can change the size of the logo for all of your automated emails (including: Welcome, Property Alert, Market Report emails) through the CMS tool (Pages > Home > Header Logo)
